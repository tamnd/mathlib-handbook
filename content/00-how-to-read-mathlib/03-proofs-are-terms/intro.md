# Proofs are terms

This chapter shows what a proof is in Lean, so that the reader can read the proofs of Mathlib as proofs of a textbook. Section 3.1 explains that a statement is a type and a proof is a term of that type, gives the term for each logical form, and lists the three axioms of classical logic that Mathlib uses. Section 3.2 reads tactic proofs: it explains the goal, lists the common tactics with their reading in a paper proof, and follows two proofs step by step with the goal after each step.

The chapter needs Chapter 2, and the logic of a first course: implication, the quantifiers, proof by contradiction and proof by induction. Chapter 4 uses it to find lemmas for a goal, and Chapter 5 uses it to read a full proof of Mathlib.
