# Cosets and the theorem of Lagrange

The cosets of a subgroup cut a group into parts of the same size. Section 1 defines the cosets and the index, proves the theorem of Lagrange and the tower law for indices, and compares left and right cosets. Section 2 studies the order of an element, proves that it divides the order of the group, and derives the theorems of Fermat and Euler. It shows that $A_4$ has no subgroup of order $6$, so the converse of Lagrange fails.

The chapter needs Chapter 3 and the divisibility of the integers from Volume 2. The quotient groups of Chapter 5 are sets of cosets, and the counting arguments of Chapters 6 and 8 rest on Lagrange.
