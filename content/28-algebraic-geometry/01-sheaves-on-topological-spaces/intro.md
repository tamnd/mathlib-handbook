# Sheaves on topological spaces

This chapter builds the theory of sheaves on a topological space that the rest of the volume uses. Section 1 defines presheaves with values in a category and the sheaf condition. It proves that the forms of this condition agree: unique gluing of compatible families, the equalizer of products, the limit over pairwise intersections and the official condition on the site of opens. It constructs sheaves of functions and of continuous functions from local predicates, shows that limits and pushforwards of sheaves are sheaves, and proves the adjunction between pullback and pushforward. Section 2 defines stalks and germs and proves that a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism when it is one on every stalk. It constructs the sheafification of a presheaf of sets by germs, defines the étale space, computes the stalks of skyscraper sheaves and proves their adjunction with the stalk functor. It ends with flasque sheaves, locally surjective morphisms and the points of the site of opens.

The chapter needs open sets, closures and continuous maps from Volume 13, with the separation axioms for the specialization of points. It uses categories, limits, colimits, Kan extensions and adjunctions from Volume 24, and the Grothendieck topologies and sheaves on sites of Volume 25.

Chapter 2 puts a sheaf of rings on a space and asks for local rings as stalks, which gives the locally ringed spaces. The stalk maps of a morphism are built from the stalk of a pushforward of this chapter. Chapter 3 builds the structure sheaf of the spectrum of a ring by a local predicate and computes its stalks as localizations. Skyscraper sheaves return with the points of schemes.
