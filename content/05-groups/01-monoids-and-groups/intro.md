# Monoids and groups

This chapter gives the axioms of a group in the form that Mathlib uses, as the end of a chain of classes from a semigroup through a monoid. Section 1 proves the first identities from the axioms: the right inverse, cancellation, and the inverse of a product. It also shows how the attribute `to_additive` gives each lemma its additive copy. Section 2 covers powers and the laws of exponents, the group of units of a monoid, and the first examples: $(\mathbb{Z}/n)^\times$ and the dihedral groups.

The chapter needs only the classes and the notation of Volume 1. Each later chapter uses its lemmas and its examples.
