# Groups with zero

A group with zero is a monoid with an absorbing element $0$ in which each nonzero element has an inverse. Fields, the extended nonnegative reals and the value groups of valuations have this structure. This chapter gives the library of monoids and groups with zero, which Volume 6 uses for rings and fields. Section 1 gives the classes of monoids and groups with zero, the zero product and cancellation lemmas, powers of zero, the convention $0^{-1} = 0$, the class NeZero, and the forms that the lemmas on commuting elements, centers and idempotents take in a group with zero. Section 2 identifies the units of a group with zero with its nonzero elements, gives the division lemmas and the ring inverse `Ring.inverse`, and counts the units of a finite group with zero. Section 3 gives the homomorphisms of monoids with zero, the group with zero `WithZero G` with its universal property and the value group of a homomorphism, and the products, functions, opposites, transferred structures and indicators of monoids with zero. Section 4 studies left, right and two-sided regular elements, regularity for an action, and the submonoid of non-zero-divisors. Section 5 gives the divisibility theory of a commutative monoid with zero: associated elements and the monoid `Associates M`, prime and irreducible elements, prime powers and squarefree elements. Section 6 gives the actions that respect zero and addition, the action of a group with zero, and the pointwise actions on sets, finite sets, submonoids and subgroups.

The chapter needs Chapters 1, 2, 3 and 6.
