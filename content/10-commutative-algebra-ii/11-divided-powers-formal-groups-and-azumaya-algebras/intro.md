# Divided powers, formal groups and Azumaya algebras

This chapter collects three structures of commutative algebra that appear in arithmetic geometry. Divided powers give a meaning to $x^n/n!$ on an ideal of a ring that is not a $\mathbb{Q}$-algebra. Formal group laws are power series in two variables that act like the addition of a group near the identity. Azumaya algebras are the algebras over a commutative ring that are locally matrix algebras.

Section 1 treats divided powers: the axioms, the examples over $\mathbb{Q}$, on nilpotent ideals and on $p\mathbb{Z}_p$, the morphisms, the sub divided power ideals and quotients, and the divided power algebra of a module with its universal property. Section 2 treats one dimensional formal group laws, the additive and multiplicative examples and the monoid of points, and then the definition of Azumaya algebras, the matrix algebras and the central property of free Azumaya algebras.

The chapter uses the ideals and power series of Volume 9 and the tensor products of Volume 8. The finite and projective modules of Chapter 9 appear in the definition of an Azumaya algebra.

In Mathlib the main notions are `DividedPowers I` for an ideal $I$, `DividedPowerAlgebra R M`, `FormalGroup R` and `IsAzumaya R A`.
