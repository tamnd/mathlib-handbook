# Orthogonal projection and the Riesz representation

This chapter proves the theorems that make Hilbert spaces simpler than Banach spaces. Section 1 proves that a complete convex set has a unique point nearest to a given vector, by the parallelogram law, and uses it to construct the orthogonal projection onto a complete subspace $K$, with $E = K \oplus K^\perp$ and $K^{\perp\perp} = \overline{K}$. Section 2 proves the representation theorem of Riesz: each bounded functional on a Hilbert space is $\langle y, \cdot \rangle$ for a unique $y$, and the map $y \mapsto \langle y, \cdot \rangle$ is a conjugate linear isometry onto the dual. It also proves the theorem of Lax and Milgram. Section 3 defines the adjoint of a bounded operator, proves $\|A^*A\| = \|A\|^2$, and proves the spectral theorem for a symmetric operator on a finite dimensional inner product space.

The chapter needs Chapters 1, 2, 4 and 6. Section 3 also uses the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of Volume 7.
