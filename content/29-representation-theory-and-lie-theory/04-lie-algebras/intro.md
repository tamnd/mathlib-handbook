# Lie algebras

This chapter sets up the algebraic theory of Lie algebras over a commutative ring. Section 1 defines Lie rings, Lie algebras and Lie modules, makes each associative algebra a Lie algebra by the commutator, identifies a Lie module with a representation into the endomorphisms, and builds the classical matrix algebras $\mathfrak{sl}_n$, $\mathfrak{so}_n$ and $\mathfrak{sp}_{2l}$, with the bracket table of $\mathfrak{sl}_2$. Section 2 studies subalgebras, ideals, the bracket of two ideals, quotients and the first isomorphism theorem, normalizers and self-normalizing subalgebras, products and direct sums, and proves that $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ is simple away from characteristic $2$. Section 3 treats derivations and the ideal of inner derivations, the exponential of a nilpotent derivation, the universal enveloping algebra and free Lie algebras with their universal properties, tensor products of Lie modules, base change, semidirect products and extensions.

The chapter needs modules, linear maps and matrices from Volume 7, algebras over a commutative ring from Volume 6, and tensor products, tensor algebras and bilinear forms from Volume 8. No analysis is needed: Lie groups do not appear, and the bracket is a purely algebraic operation.

Chapter 5 uses the bracket of ideals to build the derived series and the lower central series, and it proves Engel's theorem with normalizers from Section 2. It then studies the Killing form, which is the trace form of the adjoint representation of Section 1, and semisimple Lie algebras, whose derivations are all inner. Chapter 6 uses the self-normalizing subalgebras of Section 2 to define Cartan subalgebras, and the later chapters build root systems from them.
