# Seminorms and locally convex spaces

Many spaces of analysis have a natural topology that no single norm gives: the continuous functions with uniform convergence on compact sets, the smooth functions, and each space with a weak topology. These spaces have instead a family of seminorms, and their topology is the coarsest one that makes all the seminorms continuous. This chapter gives the theory of such spaces in the form that Mathlib uses. Section 1 defines seminorms on groups and on vector spaces, their lattice and their balls, and proves that a seminorm is continuous if and only if one of its balls is a neighborhood of $0$. Section 2 studies balanced and absolutely convex sets and the topology of a family of seminorms, with the predicate `WithSeminorms` and the classes of polynormable and normable spaces. Section 3 defines the bounded sets of a topological vector space, proves that a linear map from a metrizable space is continuous if it maps bounded sets to bounded sets, and studies Montel spaces and the topologies on spaces of operators. Section 4 studies weak topologies, the theorem of Mazur on weakly closed convex sets, polars, the weak operator topology and von Neumann algebras.

The chapter needs Chapters 1, 3, 5 and 6, and the topological vector spaces of Volume 14. A normed space is the simplest example of each notion, so the reader can read each section with a normed space in mind.
