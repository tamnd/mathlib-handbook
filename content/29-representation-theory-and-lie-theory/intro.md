# Representation theory and Lie theory

This volume teaches the representations of groups and the structure theory of Lie algebras, and their form in Mathlib. A representation of a group $G$ is an action of $G$ on a vector space by linear maps. It turns questions about a group into questions of linear algebra. A Lie algebra is a vector space with a bracket that satisfies the Jacobi identity. Lie algebras come from the tangent spaces of Lie groups and from the commutators of matrices. The two subjects meet in the theory of semisimple Lie algebras, where the representations of $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ give the root system, and the root system determines the algebra.

Chapter 1 defines representations, intertwining maps and the category `Rep k G`. Chapter 2 proves the theorem of Maschke for finite groups and studies characters, with the character table of the symmetric group $S_3$ as the main example. Chapter 3 constructs induced and coinduced representations, proves the reciprocity of Frobenius in the form of adjunctions, and treats continuous representations of topological groups.

Chapter 4 gives the axioms of Lie algebras, the classical matrix algebras, ideals, quotients, derivations, the universal enveloping algebra and free Lie algebras. Chapter 5 treats solvable and nilpotent Lie algebras, proves the theorem of Engel, and defines the Killing form and semisimple Lie algebras. Chapter 6 constructs Cartan subalgebras and weight spaces, and it shows that the roots of a semisimple Lie algebra form a root system. Chapter 7 studies root systems as objects in their own right: reflections, the Weyl group, bases, Cartan matrices, the root system $G_2$, and the construction of a Lie algebra from a root system. Chapter 8 treats Coxeter groups, and it ends with Lie algebra cochains, Lie-Rinehart algebras and vertex operators.

The volume needs groups and group actions from Volume 5, linear algebra from Volume 7, tensor products and bilinear forms from Volume 8, and the categories, functors and adjunctions of Volume 24. Chapter 3 uses the topological groups of Volume 14. Volume 26, Chapter 8 uses the category `Rep k G` for the cohomology of groups. Chapters 1 to 3 form the first part, and Chapters 4 to 7 form the second part. Each part can be read without the other. Chapter 8 uses Chapter 7 for its examples. The level is that of a graduate course. The references are Serre, *Linear Representations of Finite Groups*, Fulton and Harris, *Representation Theory*, Humphreys, *Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory*, and Bourbaki, *Lie Groups and Lie Algebras*, Chapters 4 to 6.

In Mathlib, a representation is a term of `Representation k G V`, which is a monoid homomorphism from $G$ to the linear endomorphisms of $V$. The bracket of a Lie algebra is written `⁅x, y⁆`. A root system is a term of `RootPairing ι R M N` with a spanning condition, where the roots live in $M$ and the coroots in the dual module $N$. The volume has no Lean companion yet, so the sections show the Mathlib statements and give no Lean exercises.
