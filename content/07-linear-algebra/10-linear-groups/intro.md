# Linear groups

The invertible linear maps of a module to itself make a group, and so do the invertible matrices of a fixed size. These groups and their subgroups are the classical groups. This chapter gives the forms that Mathlib has. Section 1 defines the general linear group of a module and the group `GL n R` of invertible matrices, with the determinant as a group homomorphism, the matrices with positive determinant, the center and the projective general linear group, the number of elements over a finite field, and the classification of $2 \times 2$ matrices by their discriminant. Section 2 defines the special linear group of matrices and of modules, finds its center, proves that `SL(2, F)` is generated by transvections and that `SL(2, ℤ)` is generated by two matrices, and defines the projective special linear group, the symplectic group and the unitary and orthogonal groups.

The chapter needs the determinant of Chapter 4 and the groups, subgroups and quotients of Volume 5.
