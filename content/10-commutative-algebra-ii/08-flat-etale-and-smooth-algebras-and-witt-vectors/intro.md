# Flat, étale and smooth algebras, and Witt vectors

The last chapter collects properties of algebras that come from algebraic geometry. A flat module is one for which tensor product keeps injective maps injective; localizations, free and projective modules are flat, and over a Dedekind domain flat means torsion-free. The module of Kähler differentials $\Omega_{A/R}$ is the universal target of derivations, and it is the algebraic form of the cotangent bundle.

Section 1 treats flat modules and Kähler differentials, with the two exact sequences for $\Omega$. Section 2 uses them to define unramified, smooth and étale algebras through the infinitesimal lifting criteria, and proves that étale means unramified and smooth, that localizations and finite separable field extensions are étale, and that smooth algebras are flat. The section ends with the ring of Witt vectors $W(k)$ of a perfect field $k$ of characteristic $p$, a DVR of characteristic $0$ with residue field $k$, and its isomorphism $W(\mathbb{F}_p) \cong \mathbb{Z}_p$. Section 3 collects faithfully flat modules and algebras, the descent of injectivity and surjectivity, the equational criterion, flat base change of kernels, the local nature of flatness and the rank of a finite flat algebra. Section 4 treats derivations: the calculus rules, the module and Lie algebra of derivations, differential rings, the link with lifts to square zero extensions, and the dual numbers. Section 5 treats standard étale algebras, the étale locus, the Kähler differentials of an étale map, descent of étaleness, and the category of finite étale algebras with its fiber functor. Section 6 gives extensions of algebras, the naive cotangent complex $I/I^2 \to S \otimes_P \Omega_{P/R} \to \Omega_{S/R}$ with its first homology $H_1$, and its behaviour under base change and localization. Section 7 gives families of generators, presentations and submersive presentations of algebras, the Jacobian and its product formula for composites, and the descent of a presentation to a finitely generated subring. Section 8 treats standard smooth algebras, the theorem that a smooth algebra is locally standard smooth, the smooth locus, the fiber criterion and smooth base change of integral closure. Section 9 treats unramified field extensions and local rings, the unramified locus, quasi-finite algebras, Zariski's main theorem and totally split algebras.

The chapter uses the local rings of Chapter 2, the DVRs of Chapter 3, the Dedekind domains of Chapter 4 and Hensel's lemma of Chapter 6. Tensor products are in Volume 8, and separable field extensions are in Volume 11.

In Mathlib the main classes are `Module.Flat R M`, `Algebra.Smooth R A`, `Algebra.Etale R A` and `Algebra.FormallyUnramified R A`, and the Witt vectors are `WittVector p R`, with notation `𝕎 R`.
