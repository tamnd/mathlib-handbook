# Permutations and the sign

The permutations of a finite set form the group `Equiv.Perm α`. Section 1 defines the sign by inversions, proves that it is a homomorphism, shows that the transpositions generate, and defines the alternating group $A_n$ of index $2$. Section 2 proves the cycle decomposition, computes the order and the sign from the cycle type, shows that conjugacy in $S_n$ is equality of cycle types, and proves that the $3$-cycles generate $A_n$. Section 3 describes the library under the cycle decomposition: supports and disjoint permutations, the cycle through a point and the cycle factors, the cycle of a list, and the concrete permutations of `Fin n` and `Option α`. Section 4 computes the centralizer of a permutation and the sizes of the conjugacy classes, decides when a class of $S_n$ splits in $A_n$, gives small generating sets of $S_n$, proves that the stabilizer of a subset is maximal, studies the Klein four-group of $A_4$, and proves that $A_n$ is simple for $n \ge 5$.

The chapter needs Chapters 4 to 6. The groups $S_4$ and $A_4$ are the main examples of Chapter 8, and the proof in Section 4 that $A_n$ is simple for $n \ge 5$ starts from the $3$-cycles.
