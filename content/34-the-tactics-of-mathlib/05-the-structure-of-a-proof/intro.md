# The structure of a proof

This chapter is about the tactics that give a proof its shape: `rcases`, `obtain` and `rintro` take hypotheses apart, `choose` makes a function from a hypothesis `∀ x, ∃ y`, `lift` moves a variable to a smaller type, and `filter_upwards` combines facts that hold eventually. They do no computation. They do the steps that a written proof says with "let", "take" and "for large n".
