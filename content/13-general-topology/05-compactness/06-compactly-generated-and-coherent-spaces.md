## Compactly generated and coherent spaces

### Overview

In algebraic topology, the category of all topological spaces has a defect: the product of two quotient maps need not be a quotient map, and the space of maps $C(X, Y)$ does not have a good exponential law. The usual remedy is to work with spaces whose topology is determined by compact pieces. This section gives the three forms of this idea in Mathlib.

A family $S$ of subsets of $X$ is *coherent* with the topology if a set $U$ is open as soon as $U \cap s$ is open in $s$ for each $s \in S$. A space is *compactly coherent* if it is coherent with its family of compact sets. A space is *compactly generated* if its topology is coinduced by all continuous maps from compact Hausdorff spaces into it. For a Hausdorff space the two conditions agree. A space is *Δ-generated* if its topology is coinduced by continuous maps from the spaces $\mathbb{R}^n$.

Sequential spaces and Hausdorff locally compact spaces are compactly generated, so the conditions hold for metric spaces and manifolds. A continuous map out of a compactly generated space is continuous as soon as it is continuous on each compact piece. That is the main use of the conditions.

The section uses compact sets from Section 1, local compactness from Section 4, subspace and quotient topologies from Chapter 1, and sequential spaces from Chapter 2. The general notion of a space generated by a family of spaces is in Section 5 of Chapter 1.

### The library around it

#### Coherent families of sets

The structure `Topology.IsCoherentWith S` is in `Mathlib.Topology.Defs.Induced`. Its one field says that a set $u$ is open if, for each $s \in S$, the preimage of $u$ in the subtype $s$ is open. The theory is in `Mathlib.Topology.Coherent`.

- `Topology.IsCoherentWith.isOpen_iff` and `Topology.IsCoherentWith.isClosed_iff`: a set is open, or closed, if and only if its trace on each $s \in S$ is open, or closed, in $s$;
- `Topology.IsCoherentWith.continuous_iff`: a map $f$ on $X$ is continuous if and only if it is continuous on each $s \in S$;
- `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_isClosed` and `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_continuous_prop`: two ways to prove coherence;
- `Topology.IsCoherentWith.mono` and `Topology.IsCoherentWith.enlarge`: a larger family, or a family whose members contain the old members, is also coherent;
- `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_nhds`: a family that contains a neighborhood of each point is coherent;
- `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_seq`: in a sequential space, a family that contains the set $\{x\} \cup \{u_n\}$ for each convergent sequence $u_n \to x$ is coherent.

The last two results give the two standard examples. In a weakly locally compact space each point has a compact neighborhood, so the compact sets are coherent by `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_nhds`. In a sequential space each convergent sequence with its limit is compact, so the compact sets are coherent by `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_seq`.

**Theorem.** Let $S$ be coherent with the topology of $X$. Then $f : X \to Y$ is continuous if and only if, for each $s \in S$, $f$ is continuous on $s$.

*Proof sketch.* One direction is the restriction of a continuous map. For the other, let $V \subseteq Y$ be open. For each $s \in S$, the trace of $f^{-1}(V)$ on $s$ is the preimage of $V$ under the restriction $f|_s$, so it is open in $s$. By coherence $f^{-1}(V)$ is open.

#### Compactly coherent spaces

The module `Mathlib.Topology.Compactness.CompactlyCoherentSpace` defines the class `CompactlyCoherentSpace X`, with the field `CompactlyCoherentSpace.isCoherentWith` for the family `{K | IsCompact K}`. The forms that are used in proofs are `CompactlyCoherentSpace.isOpen_iff`, `CompactlyCoherentSpace.isClosed_iff` and `CompactlyCoherentSpace.isOpen_iff_forall_compactSpace`, which uses maps from compact spaces in place of compact subsets. The instances are:

- `CompactlyCoherentSpace.of_weaklyLocallyCompactSpace`: each weakly locally compact space;
- `CompactlyCoherentSpace.of_sequentialSpace`: each sequential space, so each first countable space and each metric space.

Each space has a compactly coherent form. The type synonym `CompactCoherentification X`, with the scoped notation `𝐤`, carries the finest topology in which the inclusion of each compact subset of $X$ is continuous. The bijection is `CompactCoherentification.mk X`. The main facts are:

- `CompactCoherentification.continuous_mk_symm`: the identity from `𝐤 X` to $X$ is continuous;
- `CompactCoherentification.continuous_mk`: the identity from $X$ to `𝐤 X` is continuous when $X$ is compactly coherent, so `CompactCoherentification.homeo` is then a homeomorphism;
- `CompactCoherentification.isCompact_iff`: the two topologies have the same compact sets;
- `CompactCoherentification.continuous_dom_iff`: a map from `𝐤 X` is continuous if and only if it is continuous on each compact subset of $X$;
- `CompactCoherentification.instCompactlyCoherentSpace` and `CompactCoherentification.t2space`.

#### Compactly generated spaces

The module `Mathlib.Topology.Compactness.CompactlyGeneratedSpace` uses maps from compact Hausdorff spaces in place of compact subsets. The topology `TopologicalSpace.compactlyGenerated X` is the finest topology for which each continuous map from a compact Hausdorff space into $X$ stays continuous. There are two classes:

- `UCompactlyGeneratedSpace.{u} X`: the topology of $X$ is at least as fine as the compactly generated topology, with test spaces in the universe $u$. Since the compactly generated topology is always finer than the given one, the condition says that the two topologies agree. This form is for categorical use and needs an explicit universe;
- `CompactlyGeneratedSpace X`: the same condition with test spaces in the universe of $X$. This is the form for topology.

The main use is `continuous_from_compactlyGeneratedSpace`: a map $f$ on a compactly generated space is continuous if $f \circ g$ is continuous for each continuous $g : K \to X$ with $K$ compact Hausdorff. The open and closed forms are `CompactlyGeneratedSpace.isOpen` and `CompactlyGeneratedSpace.isClosed`. For a Hausdorff space the test maps can be replaced by compact subsets, by `CompactlyGeneratedSpace.isClosed_iff_of_t2` and `compactlyGeneratedSpace_of_isClosed_of_t2`.

The two classes are linked by `to_compactlyCoherentSpace`, which gives a compactly coherent space from a compactly generated one, and `of_compactlyCoherentSpace_of_t2`, which gives the converse for a Hausdorff space.

The instances and transfer results are:

- `instUCompactlyGeneratedSpaceOfSequentialSpace`: sequential spaces;
- `instCompactlyGeneratedSpaceOfWeaklyLocallyCompactSpace`: weakly locally compact Hausdorff spaces;
- `instUCompactlyGeneratedSpaceOfDiscreteTopology`: discrete spaces;
- `instUCompactlyGeneratedSpaceQuotient`, `instUCompactlyGeneratedSpaceSum` and `instUCompactlyGeneratedSpaceSigma`: quotients, sums and sigma types;
- `compactlyGeneratedSpace_of_coinduced`: the target of a quotient map from a compactly generated space.

There is no instance for products. The product of two compactly generated spaces with the product topology is compactly generated when the spaces are Hausdorff and one factor is locally compact, but not in general.

#### Δ-generated spaces

The module `Mathlib.Topology.Compactness.DeltaGeneratedSpace` defines `DeltaGeneratedSpace Y` as `Topology.IsGeneratedBy (fun n ↦ Fin n → ℝ) Y`, an instance of the generated spaces of Chapter 1. The two instances of the module are `DeltaGeneratedSpace.instSequentialSpace` and `DeltaGeneratedSpace.instLocallyPathConnectedSpace`. With the sequential space instance, a Δ-generated space is compactly generated. The abbreviation `DeltaGeneratedSpace.of Y` is the type $Y$ with the Δ-generated topology.

#### Common errors

1. Using `UCompactlyGeneratedSpace X` without a universe. Lean then chooses a universe variable, and the instance search can fail. Use `CompactlyGeneratedSpace X` in topology, and write `UCompactlyGeneratedSpace.{u} X` with the universe when it is necessary.
2. Assuming that compactly coherent and compactly generated agree for all spaces. The instance `of_compactlyCoherentSpace_of_t2` needs `T2Space X`. Without it, only the direction `to_compactlyCoherentSpace` is available.
3. Expecting a product instance. A product of compactly generated spaces is not compactly generated in general, so Lean finds no instance for `X × Y` from the instances on the factors alone. For metric spaces the instance comes from `SequentialSpace` and not from the factors. Use `𝐤 (X × Y)` or prove the condition from local compactness.

### Exercises

1. (A) Show that each metric space is compactly generated. Find the chain of instances through `SequentialSpace`.
2. (A) Use `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_nhds` to prove that the family of all open sets of $X$ is coherent with the topology of $X$.
3. (B) Let $X$ be compactly generated and let $f : X \to Y$ be continuous on each compact subset of $X$. Prove that $f$ is continuous when $X$ is Hausdorff, with `CompactlyGeneratedSpace.isClosed_iff_of_t2`.
4. (B) Prove that the quotient of a compactly generated space is compactly generated, with `compactlyGeneratedSpace_of_coinduced`.
5. (B) Show that the identity map from `𝐤 X` to $X$ is continuous, and that it is a homeomorphism when $X$ is weakly locally compact.
6. (C) Prove `Topology.IsCoherentWith.of_seq` from the characterization of closed sets in a sequential space by limits of sequences.
