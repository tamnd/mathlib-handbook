# Localization of monoids

The localization of a commutative monoid $M$ at a submonoid $S$ makes the elements of $S$ invertible. This chapter gives the library of localizations of monoids, the Grothendieck group and the Ore localization of a noncommutative monoid. Section 1 describes localization maps of commutative monoids and the type `Localization S`, with the arithmetic of fractions, the universal property, the uniqueness up to isomorphism and the functoriality of localization, and facts on cardinality and finite generation. Section 2 describes the localization away from an element, units, zero and unique factorization in a localization, the order on the localization of an ordered cancellative monoid, the Grothendieck group, and the Ore localization of a noncommutative monoid with its universal property and cardinality bounds.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 to 6 and the groups with zero of Chapter 9, and Section 2 also uses the order theory of Volume 4.
