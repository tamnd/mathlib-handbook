# Grothendieck topologies on schemes

This chapter puts Grothendieck topologies on the category of schemes and on the small sites of a scheme. Section 1 gives the recipe of Mathlib: a property of morphisms defines the precoverage of jointly surjective families with that property, and a quasi-compactness condition can be added. It proves that these covers are stable under base change, defines the Zariski, fppf and fpqc topologies, and compares them. It proves that representable functors are Zariski and fpqc sheaves through the equalizer for faithfully flat ring maps and a criterion that reduces fpqc sheaves to single affine morphisms. It treats the affine Zariski site, where covers are sets of sections that generate the unit ideal, and the gluing of a Zariski sheaf into a scheme. Section 2 defines the big and small étale sites and the affine étale site, proves that geometric points lift along étale morphisms and detect covers, defines the pro-étale topology of weakly étale covers, proves that continuous maps to a space form an fpqc sheaf, and defines $\ell$-adic cohomology.

The chapter needs sites, sieves and sheaves from Volume 25, flat and étale algebras and localization from Volumes 9 and 10, separable closures from Volume 11, and the $\ell$-adic integers from Volume 12. From this volume it uses schemes, open immersions, fiber products and the classes of morphisms of Chapters 3 and 5.

The sites of this chapter are the setting for cohomology of schemes with coefficients other than quasi-coherent sheaves, and the derived functors of Volume 26 give the $\ell$-adic cohomology groups. The density of open sets and the function field return in Chapter 7, where rational maps and divisors are defined on integral schemes.
