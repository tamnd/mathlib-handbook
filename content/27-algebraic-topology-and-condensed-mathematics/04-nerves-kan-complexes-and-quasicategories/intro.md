# Nerves, Kan complexes and quasicategories

This chapter studies the simplicial sets in which horns have fillers. Section 1 defines the nerve of a category and the strict Segal condition. It proves that a nerve is strict Segal, that each inner horn in a nerve has exactly one filler, that the nerve functor is fully faithful, and that the homotopy category is its left adjoint. It then defines quasicategories and inner fibrations, and reads the composition of edges up to homotopy, the coherent isomorphism, the strict bicategory of quasicategories and the simplicial nerve. Section 2 defines Kan complexes, path components, the fundamental groupoid and homotopies of maps. It proves that in a Kan complex the edges give an equivalence relation and are invertible. It defines anodyne and inner anodyne extensions, proves their closure properties, proves that a regular pairing gives an anodyne extension, and states the pushout-product theorem.

The chapter needs categories, functors, limits and adjunctions from Volume 24, Chapters 1, 2, 4 and 5, and the classes of morphisms and lifting properties of Volume 25, Chapter 3. It uses the standard simplices, horns, boundaries and the Eilenberg-Zilber lemma of Chapter 3.

Chapter 5 uses simplicial sets to define singular homology and proves the Dold-Kan correspondence. Chapter 6 places Kan complexes in the theory of model categories: the horn inclusions generate the trivial cofibrations of the Kan-Quillen model structure, and the anodyne extensions of Section 2 are its trivial cofibrations once that structure is complete. Quasicategories give the model of higher categories that the library builds on the nerve of Section 1.
