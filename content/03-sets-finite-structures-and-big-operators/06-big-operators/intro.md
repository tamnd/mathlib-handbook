# Big operators

A finite sum $\sum_{i \in s} f(i)$ and a finite product $\prod_{i \in s} f(i)$ are the big operators. Mathlib defines them over a finset, with the notation `∑ i ∈ s, f i` and `∏ i ∈ s, f i`. This chapter gives the definitions, the induction on `Finset.range` and the main rules of computation.
