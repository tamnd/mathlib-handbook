# Finding a declaration

This chapter shows how to find a lemma of Mathlib from its statement, and how to read it once it is found. Section 4.1 gives the naming convention: the words for the symbols and the signs, the order of conclusion and hypotheses, the namespaces, dot notation and the names made by `to_additive`. It decodes the names of many lemmas and proves some of them on paper. Section 4.2 gives the tools for the case when the name is not clear: the search tactics, the search engines and the commands that show a declaration. It then reads a file of Mathlib, the file on factorials, line by line.

The chapter needs Chapters 2 and 3. After it the reader can find most lemmas for a goal in a few minutes, and read the proof of a lemma in the source.
