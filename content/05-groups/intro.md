# Groups

This volume teaches group theory and its form in Mathlib, from the axioms to the Sylow theorems, and then the library of free, abelian, solvable and nilpotent groups. Each theorem of the theory sections of Chapters 1 to 8 has a complete proof on paper, the Mathlib name of the result, and computed examples in small groups: $\mathbb{Z}/n$, the Klein group, $S_3$, $S_4$, $A_4$ and the dihedral groups.

Chapters 1 to 3 give the language: monoids, groups and units, homomorphisms and isomorphisms, subgroups and cyclic groups. Chapter 4 proves the theorem of Lagrange and studies the order of an element. Chapter 5 builds quotient groups and proves the three isomorphism theorems. Chapter 6 introduces group actions, with orbit-stabilizer and the class equation. Chapter 7 studies permutations, the sign and the cycle type. Chapter 8 proves the theorem of Cauchy and the three Sylow theorems, and applies them to groups of small order. Chapter 9 gives the library of monoids and groups with zero: units and division, homomorphisms, regular elements, associated and prime elements, and actions that respect zero. Chapter 10 builds free groups, free products and presented groups, with HNN extensions and amalgamated products. Chapter 11 studies torsion, divisible, free and finite abelian groups. Chapter 12 studies solvable, nilpotent and perfect groups, and builds groups from a normal subgroup and a quotient by semidirect products, extensions and wreath products. Chapter 13 localizes monoids, with the Grothendieck group and the Ore localization. Chapter 14 gives the pointwise products, inverses and actions on sets and finite sets of a group, and applies them to submonoids and subgroups.

Read Volume 1 first: this volume uses its classes, sets, equivalences and finite cardinalities. The divisibility, $\gcd$ and congruences of the integers, from Volume 2, are used from Chapter 4 on. Read the chapters in order. Later volumes on rings, modules and fields use the quotients of Chapter 5, the actions of Chapter 6 and the Sylow theorems of Chapter 8. Each chapter has a companion file of Lean examples, exercises and solutions in tamnd/mathlib-lean.
