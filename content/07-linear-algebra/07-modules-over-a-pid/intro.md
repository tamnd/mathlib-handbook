# Modules over a principal ideal domain

Over a field, each finitely generated module is free, and the dimension classifies it. Over the integers this fails: $\mathbb{Z}/6$ has no basis. This chapter classifies the finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain, such as $\mathbb{Z}$ or $K[X]$. Section 1 defines the free, torsion and torsion free modules, and proves that a submodule of a free module of finite rank is free. Section 2 proves the theorem on the Smith normal form, derives the structure theorem $M \cong R^r \oplus \bigoplus_i R/(p_i^{e_i})$, and applies it to abelian groups and to explicit quotients of $\mathbb{Z}^2$.

The chapter needs the bases and quotients of Chapters 1 and 2, the determinant of Chapter 4, and the principal ideal domains of Volume 6.
