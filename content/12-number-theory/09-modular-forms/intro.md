# Modular forms

A modular form of weight $k$ for a subgroup $\Gamma$ of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is a holomorphic function $f$ on the upper half plane $\mathbb{H}$ with $f(\gamma z) = (cz + d)^k f(z)$ for each $\gamma \in \Gamma$, and with a growth condition at the cusps. Modular forms join analysis and arithmetic: their Fourier coefficients are often arithmetic functions, such as the divisor sums of Chapter 2 or the number of representations by a quadratic form. This chapter describes the part of the theory that Mathlib has. It is an api chapter: it explains the definitions and the main results of the library, with short proofs where they exist. Section 1 treats the groups: the fundamental domain of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, the arithmetic and congruence subgroups, the cusps with their widths, and the abstract Hecke rings. Section 2 defines the slash action, modular forms and cusp forms, and gives the $q$-expansion, the bounds on its coefficients, the Petersson product, the change of level and the Serre derivative. Section 3 constructs the Eisenstein series, computes their $q$-expansions, treats the series $E_2$ of weight $2$ and the Ramanujan equations, and defines the eta function and the discriminant $\Delta$. Section 4 computes the spaces of modular forms of level one and their graded ring $\mathbb{C}[E_4, E_6]$, defines the $L$-function of a modular form, and describes the Jacobi theta functions.

The chapter needs the holomorphic functions of Volume 19 and the groups of matrices of Volume 7. The L-series of Chapter 6 return in the L-function of a modular form.
