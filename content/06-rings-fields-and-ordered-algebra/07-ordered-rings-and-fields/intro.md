# Ordered rings and fields

This chapter studies rings and fields with an order that respects the ring operations. Section 1 defines ordered semirings and strict ordered semirings in the unbundled form that Mathlib uses, with the mixins `IsOrderedRing` and `IsStrictOrderedRing` over a separate order. It proves the rules of computation with inequalities, shows that a nontrivial ordered ring has characteristic zero and that squares are nonnegative, and concludes that $\mathbb{C}$ admits no order. It introduces positive cones, proves that $\mathbb{Z}$ has exactly one ordering, and orders $\mathbb{R}[X]$ by the sign of the leading coefficient. Section 2 turns to ordered fields: the rules for inverses and quotients, the mediant, the uniqueness of the order on $\mathbb{Q}$, the embedding of $\mathbb{Q}$ in every ordered field, the field $\mathbb{R}(x)$ with an infinite element, and formally real fields. Section 3 defines the archimedean property, proves it for $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{R}$, constructs the floor function, proves that $\mathbb{Q}$ is dense in each archimedean ordered field, and states that every archimedean ordered field embeds in $\mathbb{R}$.

The chapter needs rings, integral domains, fields and the field of fractions from Chapters 1 to 3, and the characteristic from Chapter 4 in one proof. It uses partial orders, linear orders, suprema and Galois connections from Volume 4.

Chapter 8 uses ordered rings for the star-ordered rings of Section 2 and the archimedean floor function for the continued fractions of Section 3. Volume 15 on real analysis builds the real numbers as a complete archimedean ordered field and uses the results of this chapter in each estimate.
