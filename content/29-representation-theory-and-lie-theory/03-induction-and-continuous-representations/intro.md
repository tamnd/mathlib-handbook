# Induction and continuous representations

This chapter moves representations between groups and adds a topology to them. Section 1 defines the coinvariants of a representation, the largest quotient with trivial action, and shows that they are left adjoint to the trivial action functor. It builds the induced representation as coinvariants of $k[H] \otimes A$ and the coinduced representation as a module of functions, and proves Frobenius reciprocity in its two forms: induction is left adjoint to restriction, and coinduction is right adjoint to it. For a subgroup of finite index the two constructions agree, and the section proves this isomorphism with explicit maps. It closes with stabilizers of vectors and with Schur's lemma for a simple module over an algebra over an algebraically closed field. Section 2 treats continuous representations, the category `TopRep k G` and its equivalence with actions on topological modules, the module $C(G, V)$ that underlies continuous cohomology, and examples from the circle and from profinite groups.

The chapter needs the representations and the category `Rep k G` of Chapter 1, and the characters of Chapter 2 for one example. It uses cosets, normal subgroups and group actions from Volume 5, tensor products from Volume 8, adjunctions from Volume 24 and the categories of modules and representations from Volume 26, Chapters 7 and 8. Section 2 uses topological groups and topological modules from Volume 14 and the compact-open topology from Volume 13.

Induction and coinduction are the input of Shapiro's lemma in Volume 26, Chapter 8, and the coinduction $C(G, V)$ defines continuous group cohomology. Schur's lemma for algebras is also the tool for irreducible modules of Lie algebras in Chapters 4 to 6, where an abelian Lie algebra acts on a simple finite dimensional module by scalars.
