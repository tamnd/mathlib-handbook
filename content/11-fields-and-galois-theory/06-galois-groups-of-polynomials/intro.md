# Galois groups of polynomials

Chapter 5 attached a group to a Galois extension. This chapter attaches a group to a polynomial: the Galois group of $f \in K[X]$ is the automorphism group of a splitting field of $f$ over $K$. It permutes the roots of $f$, and this permutation action is the main tool to compute it. So the Galois group of a polynomial of degree $n$ is a subgroup of the symmetric group $S_n$, and the questions are which subgroup, and how to see it from the coefficients of $f$.

Section 1 defines the group and its action on the roots, proves that the action is transitive exactly when the polynomial is irreducible, and introduces the discriminant. The discriminant decides whether the group lies in the alternating group, and for an irreducible cubic it decides between $A_3$ and $S_3$. Section 2 computes the Galois groups of the standard examples: $X^3 - 3X + 1$ with the cyclic group of order $3$, $X^4 - 2$ with the dihedral group of order $8$, and $X^5 - 4X + 2$ with the full group $S_5$. The last example rests on a criterion for polynomials of prime degree over $\mathbb{Q}$, and Chapter 8 uses it to give a quintic that is not solvable by radicals.

The chapter needs the fundamental theorem (Chapter 5), and from Volume 5 the symmetric groups, the sign and the alternating groups (Chapter 7), and group actions (Chapter 6).
