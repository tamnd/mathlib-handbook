# Valuations, divisors and primality

This chapter measures divisibility. Section 1 defines the $p$-adic valuation $v_p(n)$, the exponent of the prime $p$ in the factorization of $n$, on the natural numbers, the integers and the rationals. It proves that $v_p$ turns products into sums and that $v_p(a + b) \geq \min(v_p(a), v_p(b))$. It then computes $v_p(n!)$, $v_p$ of a binomial coefficient, and $v_p(a^n - b^n)$. Section 2 studies the set of divisors of a number, the sum of the divisors and the perfect numbers, and then the question of how to prove that a large number is prime. It treats pseudoprimes, Lucas's test and the Lucas-Lehmer test.

The chapter needs the divisibility, the greatest common divisor and the unique factorization of Volume 2, Chapter 2, the congruences of Volume 2, Chapter 3, and the units of `ZMod n` with Fermat's little theorem and Euler's theorem from Volume 2, Chapter 6. The primality tests use the order of an element of a finite group from Volume 5, Chapter 4.

The valuation returns in Chapter 5, where the $p$-adic norm $|x|_p = p^{-v_p(x)}$ gives the field $\mathbb{Q}_p$. The divisor sums of Section 2 return in Chapter 2.
