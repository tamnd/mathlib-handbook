# Extreme points, cones and polytopes

This chapter studies convex sets from their boundary. A compact convex set is determined by its extreme points. A cone is determined by its faces and its dual cone. A polytope is determined by its vertices and can be cut into simplices.

Section 1 defines extreme and exposed sets and the intrinsic interior, proves the theorem of Minkowski in $\mathbb{R}^d$, and states the theorem of Krein and Milman as Mathlib proves it. Section 2 develops the algebraic theory of cones: the dual cone along a pairing, the duals of finite sets, faces and simplicial cones. Section 3 treats simplicial complexes, polygons, the simplicial set of affine simplices in a convex space, and the first facts about polytopes.

The chapter needs Chapters 2, 6 and 7 and the separation theorems of Volume 16, Chapter 5. Each section says what the index lacks: the theorem of Minkowski and Weyl, the algebraic Farkas lemma, and a theory of polytopes and their faces.
