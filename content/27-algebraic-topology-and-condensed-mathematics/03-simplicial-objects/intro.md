# Simplicial objects

This chapter builds the combinatorial side of homotopy theory. Section 1 defines the simplex category $\Delta$ of finite nonempty ordinals, its faces and degeneracies, and proves the simplicial identities, the unique factorization of each map as a surjection followed by an injection, and the normal form of a morphism. It reads the presented category, the augmented simplex category with ordinal sum, the truncations and the semi-simplex category. Section 2 defines simplicial and cosimplicial objects in a category, augmentations and the Čech nerve. It proves that an extra degeneracy makes the alternating face complex contractible and that the Čech nerve of a split epimorphism has one. It then reads simplicial homotopies, splittings, coskeleta, the Reedy structure and simplicial categories. Section 3 treats simplicial sets: the standard simplices and the Yoneda lemma, boundaries and horns, the Eilenberg-Zilber lemma and the attachment of cells along boundaries.

The chapter needs categories, functors and the Yoneda lemma from Volume 24, Chapters 1 to 3, limits, colimits and Kan extensions from Chapter 4, and adjunctions from Chapter 5. Section 1 uses monoidal categories from Chapter 7. Section 2 uses the chain complexes and chain homotopies of Volume 26, Chapters 2 and 3, and Section 3 uses the classes of morphisms of Volume 25, Chapter 3.

Chapter 4 uses the horns of Section 3 to define Kan complexes and quasicategories, and it studies the nerve of a category through the strict Segal condition. Chapter 5 forms the chain complexes of a simplicial object and proves the Dold-Kan correspondence, which rests on the splittings of Section 2. Chapter 6 places simplicial sets in the theory of model categories, where the cell structure of Section 3 gives the cofibrations.
