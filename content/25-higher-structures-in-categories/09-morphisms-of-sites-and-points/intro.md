# Morphisms of sites and points

This chapter studies the functors between sites and the sheaves that they move. A continuous map of spaces gives a pullback and a pushforward of sheaves. For sites, a functor between the underlying categories gives these operations when it is continuous or cocontinuous for the topologies. The chapter also treats dense subsites, which give equivalent categories of sheaves, the site of the objects over a fixed object, and the points of a site, which generalize the stalks at the points of a space.

Section 1 defines continuous, cover preserving and cocontinuous functors, it gives the pullback and the pushforward of sheaves with their adjunctions, and it defines the topologies that a functor induces on its source. Section 2 treats dense subsites and the comparison lemma, the transport of a topology along an equivalence, and the sites over an object. Section 3 defines points of a site by fiber functors, and it gives the stalk functors, the skyscraper sheaves and the conservative families of points.

The chapter needs Chapter 6 of this volume, the object properties of Chapter 3, and the adjunctions and filtered colimits of Volume 24.
