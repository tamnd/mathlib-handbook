# Functions and equivalences

This chapter proves the standard facts about injective, surjective and bijective maps and their inverses, defines equivalences and embeddings, and proves the theorems of Cantor and of Schröder and Bernstein. It then treats relations, the correspondence of equivalence relations with partitions, and quotient types.

Four sections of reference material follow. The first lists the canonical equivalences between products, sums, options, sigma types, subtypes and sets, and the embeddings built from disjoint sets. The second gives the equivalences of `Fin n` with sums, products and options, the rotations of `Fin n`, and the encodings of lists, multisets and finite sets. The third treats partial equivalences, `PartialEquiv` and `PEquiv`. The last covers composition and pairing of functions, the iterates of a map, semiconjugate and commuting maps, and constant functions.
