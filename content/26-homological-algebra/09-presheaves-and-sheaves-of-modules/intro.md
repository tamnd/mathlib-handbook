# Presheaves and sheaves of modules

This chapter extends the category of modules of Chapter 7 from one ring to a presheaf or a sheaf of rings on a category. These categories are the frame for quasicoherent sheaves on schemes and for the cohomology of sheaves of modules. Section 1 treats presheaves of modules: the abelian structure, limits and colimits computed objectwise, free presheaves and the generators made from representable presheaves, restriction of scalars, pushforward and pullback, the tensor product over a presheaf of commutative rings, sheafification, and the presheaf of differentials. Section 2 treats sheaves of modules: the abelian structure, limits and colimits, sheafification as a localization, free sheaves and generating sections, sheaves of finite type, quasicoherent and locally free sheaves, pushforward and pullback along a continuous functor of sites, subsheaves of modules, and the module structure on the stalks of a presheaf of modules on a space.

The chapter needs categories, adjunctions and limits from Volume 24, abelian categories, localization and sites from Volume 25, and the category of modules from Chapter 7 of this volume. Section 2 uses Section 1 throughout. The later volumes on algebraic geometry use these categories for the modules over a scheme.
