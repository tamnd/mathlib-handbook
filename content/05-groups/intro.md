# Groups

This volume teaches the group theory of Mathlib, from the axioms to the Sylow theorems. It starts with monoids and groups, and it shows how one lemma gives its additive copy. Then it covers homomorphisms, subgroups, cosets and the theorem of Lagrange, normal subgroups and quotients, group actions, permutations and the theorems of Cauchy and Sylow. Read Volume 1 first, because this volume uses its classes, sets and equivalences. Read the chapters in order. Each chapter has a companion file of Lean examples and exercises in tamnd/mathlib-lean.
