# Sets, finite structures and big operators

This volume shows the collections of Mathlib and the sums and products over them. It starts with the operations on sets, then it builds the finite collections: lists, multisets, finsets and finite types. It gives the three ways to say that a collection is finite, and it shows the sums and products `Finset.sum` and `Finset.prod`. The last chapters show the finitely supported functions and the countable types. Read Volume 1 first, because this volume uses its sets, subtypes and classes.
