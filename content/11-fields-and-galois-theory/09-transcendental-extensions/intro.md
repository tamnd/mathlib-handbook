# Transcendental extensions

The earlier chapters study algebraic extensions. This chapter studies the extensions that are not algebraic. The model is the field $K(t)$ of rational functions in one variable, and more generally the field $K(t_1, \dots, t_n)$. An extension $L/K$ of any kind splits into a purely transcendental part and an algebraic part: a transcendence basis $B$ gives the tower $K \subseteq K(B) \subseteq L$, where $K(B)$ is a field of rational functions and $L/K(B)$ is algebraic. The size of $B$ is the transcendence degree.

Section 1 is the library of algebraic independence. It shows how independence behaves under adjoining one element, in towers and under extension of the base by an algebraic extension, and it computes with the transcendence degree and with the cardinality of a field of given transcendence degree. Section 2 is the library of the field `RatFunc K` of rational functions in one variable: its construction, the numerator and the denominator, evaluation, the degree, the valuation at infinity, the substitution $t \mapsto t + r$ and the theorem of Lüroth on the subfields of $K(t)$.

The chapter needs algebraic and transcendental elements and transcendence bases (Chapter 2, Section 1), the minimal polynomial (Chapter 2, Section 2) and the tower law (Chapter 1).
