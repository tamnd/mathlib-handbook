# Countable and denumerable types

After the finite types come the countable types: the types with an injective map to `ℕ`. The class `Countable` and the predicate `Set.Countable` give this property, and the classes `Encodable` and `Denumerable` give it with data. This chapter shows the closure rules, the coding of pairs and the proof of Cantor that the real numbers are not countable.
