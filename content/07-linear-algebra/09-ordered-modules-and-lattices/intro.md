# Ordered modules and lattices

This chapter studies modules with an order and modules inside a real vector space. Section 1 defines the classes that relate an order on the scalars to an order on the module, such as `PosSMulMono` and `IsOrderedModule`, and the positive linear maps. Section 2 studies the archimedean classes of an ordered module and states the embedding theorem of Hahn, which embeds an ordered vector space over an archimedean field into a space of Hahn series. Section 3 defines a lattice in a vector space, a discrete subgroup of $\mathbb{R}^n$ that spans it, with its fundamental domain and its covolume.

The chapter needs the ordered fields of Volume 4, the bases and the dimension of Chapter 2, and for Section 3 the measure of Volume 18 in a few statements.
