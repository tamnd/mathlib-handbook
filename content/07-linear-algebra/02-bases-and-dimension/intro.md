# Bases and dimension

A basis gives each vector unique coordinates, and the number of the basis vectors is the dimension. This chapter proves that the number does not depend on the basis. Section 1 defines linear independence and bases, and shows that a basis is the same as an isomorphism with a module of coordinates. Section 2 proves the exchange lemma of Steinitz, the existence of bases in vector spaces, and the invariance of dimension, and it introduces `Module.rank`. Section 3 defines the finite dimension `Module.finrank` and proves the rank-nullity theorem with its corollaries.

The chapter needs Chapter 1, the finite sums of Volume 3 and the cardinals of Volume 1. Over a field every module has a basis. Over the integers most modules do not, and the chapter marks the theorems that need a field. Chapter 3 uses bases to turn linear maps into matrices, and Chapter 7 studies the free modules over a principal ideal domain, the modules that have a basis.
