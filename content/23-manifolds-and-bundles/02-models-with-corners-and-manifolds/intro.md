# Models with corners and manifolds

A charted space with a groupoid has compatible charts, but the model space $H$ of Chapter 1 is only a topological space, and one cannot differentiate a map of $H$. This chapter embeds $H$ into a normed space $E$ through a model with corners $I : H \to E$, so that a change of charts read through $I$ is a map between subsets of $E$. A $C^n$ manifold is a charted space whose changes of charts are $C^n$ in this sense.

Section 1 defines models with corners. The image of $I$ is a closed set, convex over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, with nonempty interior, and the section proves that it is the closure of its interior. It defines the groupoid of $C^n$ maps of a model, the class `IsManifold`, and the first manifolds: normed spaces, open subsets and products. Section 2 defines the extended charts, the compositions of a chart with $I$. They take values in $E$, and all the analysis on a manifold goes through them. The section proves the formulas for their sources, targets and neighbourhoods, and the smoothness of the extended changes of charts. Section 3 gives the examples with boundary and the standard examples: the half space, the quadrant, the interval, the sphere, the units of a Banach algebra. It defines interior and boundary points, and proves with the theorem of Hahn and Banach that this notion does not depend on the chart.

The chapter needs Chapter 1, the normed spaces and finite dimensional spaces of Volume 16, Chapters 1 and 3, the theorem of Hahn and Banach of Volume 16, Chapter 5, and the classes $C^n$ of Volume 17, Chapter 4. Chapter 3 uses the extended charts to define smooth maps, and Chapter 4 uses them to define the derivative.
