# Double complexes and spectral sequences

This chapter is about complexes with two indices and about the spectral sequences that compute their cohomology. Section 1 builds graded objects, differential objects, bicomplexes and their total complexes. It proves that the total differential squares to zero under one sign condition, that complexes are differential objects in a category of graded objects, that a first quadrant bicomplex with exact columns has an acyclic total complex, and that the canonical truncation keeps the cohomology in the degrees that it keeps. Section 2 defines spectral sequences as sequences of pages with isomorphisms between the homology of a page and the next page. It then defines spectral objects in the sense of Verdier, from which Mathlib constructs its spectral sequences. It proves that the objects of the pages are subobjects of the terms of the spectral object, computes the second page, and proves that for a finite filtration the last page gives the graded pieces of the abutment.

The chapter needs the shifts and the triangulated categories of Volume 25, Chapter 5, the complexes and long exact sequences of Chapter 2, the mapping cones of Chapter 3, and the Tor groups of Chapter 4 for the examples. Section 2 uses the derived category of Chapter 5 for its motivation. The examples use small complexes of abelian groups, which the reader can check by hand.

Spectral sequences are the main computational tool in the cohomology of spaces in Volume 27, of sheaves in Volume 28, and of groups in Volume 29. The Mathlib library for them is recent, and it does not yet name the spectral sequence of a filtered complex or a theorem of convergence. The text proves the finite case by hand, and the sections say where the library stops.
