# Absolute values and seminorms on rings

The number theory of a field such as $\mathbb{Q}$ uses many absolute values on the same field at the same time, and the theory of local fields extends an absolute value from a field to its algebraic extensions. This chapter gives the analytic side of these topics. Section 1 studies ultrametric norms, with the strong triangle inequality, the open subgroups that their balls give, the test $\|n\| \le 1$ for fields, and the lemma of Krasner. Section 2 gives the type synonym `WithAbs`, which puts a chosen absolute value on a ring as its norm, and the completion of a field at an absolute value with the local degrees of an extension. Section 3 defines bundled seminorms and norms on rings and algebras, and three constructions that make a seminorm submultiplicative, power multiplicative or multiplicative at a given element. Section 4 defines the spectral norm on an algebraic extension of an ultrametric field, proves that it extends the norm of the field, and proves that it is the only power multiplicative extension when the field is complete.

The chapter needs Chapters 1, 3, 8 and 9, the algebra of fields and polynomials of the earlier volumes, the absolute values of Volume 6 and the topological algebra of Volume 14. Section 1 is the base for the other sections, and Section 4 uses the constructions of Section 3.
