# Group actions

A group action lets a group move the points of a set. Section 1 defines actions, orbits and stabilizers, proves the orbit-stabilizer theorem and the theorem of Cayley, and studies the action on cosets. Section 2 applies orbit-stabilizer to the action by conjugation: the class equation, the fixed point congruence for $p$-groups, the nontrivial center of a $p$-group, the commutativity of groups of order $p^2$, and the lemma of Burnside.

The chapter needs Chapters 4 and 5. The fixed point congruence is the main tool of the proofs of Cauchy and Sylow in Chapter 8, and Chapter 7 uses the class equation for $S_n$.
