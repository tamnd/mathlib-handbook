# Monoids and groups

This chapter gives the axioms of a group in the form that Mathlib uses, as the end of a chain of classes from a semigroup through a monoid. Section 1 proves the first identities from the axioms: the right inverse, cancellation, and the inverse of a product. It also shows how the attribute `to_additive` gives each lemma its additive copy. Section 2 covers powers and the laws of exponents, the group of units of a monoid, and the first examples: $(\mathbb{Z}/n)^\times$ and the dihedral groups. Section 3 gives the notation classes that come before the group axioms, the pointwise operations on functions and pairs, the functions `Pi.single`, the support of a function and the indicator function of a set. Section 4 builds the opposite `MulOpposite`, which reverses the product, and the symmetrized algebra `SymAlg` with the product $\frac12(ab+ba)$. Section 5 gives the API of units, invertible elements, and commuting and semiconjugate elements. Section 6 builds the min-tropical and max-tropical semirings, where the sum is the minimum or the maximum and the product is the old sum. Section 7 studies squares, idempotent elements, self-inverse elements, and even and odd functions. Section 8 gives the additive groups $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathrm{Fin}\ n$, with parity, units and congruence modulo an element. Section 9 gives the type tags `Additive` and `Multiplicative`, which turn an additive structure into a multiplicative one and back. Section 10 transfers a structure along an injection, a surjection or an equivalence, lifts it to a higher universe, and adjoins an identity to a semigroup. Section 11 gives divisibility in monoids, irreducible elements, and the ideals of a semigroup.

The chapter needs only the classes and the notation of Volume 1. Each later chapter uses its lemmas and its examples.
