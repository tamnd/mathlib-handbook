# Witt vectors and perfect rings

Chapter 8 defines the ring $W(R)$ of $p$-typical Witt vectors and proves that $W(k)$ is a DVR for a perfect field $k$ of characteristic $p$. This chapter develops the rest of the theory: the operations on Witt vectors, the truncated Witt vectors and the completeness of $W(k)$, and the perfect rings and tilts that lead to $p$-adic Hodge theory.

Section 1 treats the structure polynomials and the ghost argument, the Frobenius and the Verschiebung with their identities, the Teichmüller lifts and the Teichmüller expansion over a perfect ring, and the coordinates of a product. Section 2 treats the truncated Witt vectors and the universal property of $W(R)$ as an inverse limit, the $p$-adic completeness of $W(k)$, the Witt vectors of a domain, the Frobenius equation in the fraction field and the classification of isocrystals of dimension one. Section 3 treats the perfection of a ring of characteristic $p$, the tilt, the Teichmüller map into a complete ring, the untilt, Fontaine's map $\theta$ and the de Rham period rings.

The chapter uses the Witt vectors of Chapter 8, the valuations of Chapter 3 and the adic completions of Chapter 6. The $p$-adic numbers are in Volume 12.

In Mathlib the main notions are `WittVector.frobenius`, `WittVector.verschiebung`, `WittVector.teichmuller`, `TruncatedWittVector p n R`, `Perfection R p`, `PreTilt O p` and `WittVector.fontaineTheta`.
