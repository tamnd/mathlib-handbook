# Pointwise operations

For subsets $s$ and $t$ of a group, the product $s t$ is the set of all products $a b$ with $a \in s$ and $b \in t$. This chapter gives the library of these pointwise operations. Section 1 treats the pointwise product, inverse and power of sets. Section 2 treats the action of a group on its sets, with the translates $a s$, and the pointwise operations on unions and intersections. Sections 3 and 4 give the same two theories for finite sets, where the cardinality of a product is the main question. Section 5 applies the pointwise operations to submonoids and subgroups, and studies the actions of a group on its subgroups.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 to 3 and 6, and Section 5 also uses the normal cores of Chapter 5.
