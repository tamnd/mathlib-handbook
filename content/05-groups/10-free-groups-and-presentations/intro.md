# Free groups and presentations

A free group on a set $S$ is the group of reduced words in the letters of $S$ and their inverses. Each map from $S$ to a group extends to a unique homomorphism. A presentation describes a group as a quotient of a free group by the normal closure of a set of relations. This chapter gives the library of free products, free groups, presented groups, HNN extensions and amalgamated products. Section 1 defines the free product of two monoids and of a family of monoids, with the universal property, the normal form by reduced words and the ping-pong lemma. Section 2 builds the free group on a type from reduced words, with the universal property, the normal form and length of an element, cyclically reduced words, and the centers and centralizers of free groups. Section 3 describes free bases and the class of free groups, the Nielsen-Schreier theorem, presented groups and presentations as data, and finitely presented groups. Section 4 describes HNN extensions and amalgamated products of groups, with their universal properties, normal forms, the lemma of Britton and the injectivity of the factor maps. Section 5 builds the free magma, the free semigroup and the free monoid on a type of letters, with their universal properties, the maps between them, and the counts and sets of letters of a word.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 to 6.
