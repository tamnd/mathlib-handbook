# Affine subspaces and affine independence

This chapter studies the linear algebra of an affine space: its subspaces, the combinations of its points, the independence of families of points, and, over an ordered ring, the order of points on a line.

Section 1 defines affine subspaces, their directions and the affine span, and proves that the subspaces form a complete lattice. It also defines parallel subspaces. Section 2 defines affine combinations, proves that affine maps commute with them, and defines the centroid. It proves the theorem of Ceva. Section 3 defines affine independence, affine bases, collinear sets and simplices. It proves that an affine basis of a space of dimension $n$ has $n + 1$ points and that the medians of a simplex meet at its centroid. Section 4 defines betweenness, lists of points in order on a line, and the two sides of an affine subspace.

The chapter needs Chapter 1 and Volume 7, and Section 4 uses rays from Volume 16. Chapter 3 uses simplices and betweenness for triangles, and Chapters 6 to 8 use affine combinations for convex hulls and polytopes.
