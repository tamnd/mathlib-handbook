# The Sylow theorems

The Sylow theorems give a partial converse of the theorem of Lagrange for prime powers. Section 1 proves the theorem of Cauchy, by the proof of McKay, and the first Sylow theorem: a subgroup of each prime power order that divides $|G|$. It explains why Mathlib defines `Sylow p G` as the maximal $p$-subgroups. Section 2 proves the second and the third Sylow theorems and uses them to study groups of order $pq$, $12$, $30$ and $56$.

The chapter needs Chapters 4 to 6, and the correspondence theorem of Chapter 5. It closes the volume.
