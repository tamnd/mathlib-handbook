# Group homomorphisms

A homomorphism is a map that respects the product. Section 1 defines the bundled type `G →* H`, proves that a homomorphism preserves the unit, inverses and powers, and studies composites and isomorphisms `G ≃* H`. Section 2 covers the invariants of an isomorphism, the product of groups and the Chinese remainder theorem for cyclic groups, and the automorphism group, with conjugation and $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}/n) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/n)^\times$.

The chapter needs Chapter 1. The kernel and the image of a homomorphism are subgroups, the topic of Chapter 3, and the first isomorphism theorem in Chapter 5 describes the image.
