# Convex sets and convex functions

This chapter starts the convex half of the volume. Volume 16, Chapter 5 defines convex sets in a real vector space and proves the separation theorems. This chapter asks instead what structure the words "convex combination" need. The answer is the convex spaces of Mathlib: sets with an operation that combines finitely many points with weights that sum to $1$.

Section 1 defines convex spaces, convex sets, segments, star-convex sets and visibility, and proves that binary combinations generate all finite ones over a field. Section 2 studies convex functions: the epigraph test, the finite Jensen inequality, the maximum principle on a hull, and local minima. It proves that the finitely supported distributions on a set form the free convex space on that set.

The chapter needs linear algebra (Volume 7), topology (Volume 13), and the affine spaces and betweenness of Chapters 1 and 2. Chapter 7 builds the convex hull on the free convex space. Chapter 8 uses the maximum principle and the cone construction of Section 2.
