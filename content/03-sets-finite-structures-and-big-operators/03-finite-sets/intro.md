# Finite sets

A finset is a finite set that a program can see. Mathlib stores it as a multiset with no repeated element, so its card, its filters and its images are functions that compute. This chapter is the base of the counting in the rest of the volume.

Section 1 gives the structure `Finset α`, its operations and the first rules for the card: the card of an insert, of a subset, of an image and of the power set, and inclusion and exclusion for two finsets. Its main tool is induction on finsets. Section 2 counts the subsets of a given size. It connects the finset `Finset.powersetCard k s` with the binomial coefficient `Nat.choose`, and it proves the symmetry, the factorial formula, the sum of a row and the identity of Vandermonde by counting. Section 3 collects the rules of sum, difference, bijection and product, inclusion and exclusion for three finsets, the card of the intervals of $\mathbb{N}$, and the pigeonhole principle in its simple and strong forms.

The chapter needs the sets of Chapter 1 and the multisets of Chapter 2, and from Volume 2 the natural numbers and induction. Chapter 4 uses it for the finite types, where the finset of all elements is `Finset.univ`. Chapter 5 connects it with the predicate `Set.Finite`. Chapter 6 sums over finsets, and there the general inclusion and exclusion, double counting and the binomial theorem complete the counting of this chapter.
