# The real numbers

Mathlib builds `ℝ` from the Cauchy sequences of rational numbers. The first section gives the construction with complete proofs: the equivalence of Cauchy sequences, the operations on the classes, the order, and the proof that `ℝ` is an ordered field. The second section proves the least upper bound property from the construction, and from it the archimedean property, the density of `ℚ`, the existence of $\sqrt{2}$ and the monotone convergence theorem. It also shows the junk values of `sSup` on sets that are empty or not bounded above.
