# Hypercovers, descent and cohomology

This chapter treats the parts of the theory of sites that go past the sheaf condition. A hypercover is a cover by a family, with covers of the overlaps in a second step. It gives a form of the sheaf condition that works with families of objects instead of sieves. Descent asks when objects over the members of a cover glue to an object over the base: a stack is a pseudofunctor for which they always glue. Sheaf cohomology measures how far the global sections functor is from exact.

Section 1 treats 0-hypercovers, the precoverages that they form, and their refinements. Section 2 treats 1-hypercovers and the sheaf condition that they give. Section 3 defines descent data for a pseudofunctor, prestacks and stacks. Section 4 gives sheaf cohomology as Ext groups in the category of sheaves, with its long exact sequences and the Mayer Vietoris sequence, and it gives the first nonabelian cohomology.

The chapter needs the sites and sheaves of Chapter 6, the limits, adjunctions and monads of Volume 24, and, for Section 4, the abelian categories of Chapter 2.
