# Normal subgroups and quotients

A normal subgroup is a subgroup whose left and right cosets agree, and its cosets form a group. Section 1 defines normal subgroups and the quotient `G ⧸ N`, proves its universal property and the first isomorphism theorem, with examples such as $\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z} \cong S^1$ and $D_4/Z(D_4) \cong V_4$. Section 2 proves the second and the third isomorphism theorems and the correspondence between the subgroups of $G/N$ and the subgroups of $G$ that contain $N$. Section 3 gives commutators of elements and of subgroups, the commutator subgroup, the abelianization with its universal property, and the commuting probability of a finite group. Section 4 treats congruences, the analog of normal subgroups for monoids, with the quotient monoid, kernels, the isomorphism theorems and monoids given by generators and relations. Section 5 treats normal closures, normal cores, normalizers, characteristic subgroups and products of subgroups, with their cardinalities.

The chapter needs Chapters 2 to 4. Chapter 8 uses the correspondence theorem in the proof of the first Sylow theorem, and later volumes use quotients for rings and modules.
