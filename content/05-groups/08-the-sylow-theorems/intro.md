# The Sylow theorems

The Sylow theorems give a partial converse of the theorem of Lagrange for prime powers. Section 1 proves the theorem of Cauchy, by the proof of McKay, and the first Sylow theorem: a subgroup of each prime power order that divides $|G|$. It explains why Mathlib defines `Sylow p G` as the maximal $p$-subgroups. Section 2 proves the second and the third Sylow theorems and uses them to study groups of order $pq$, $12$, $30$ and $56$. Section 3 describes the transfer homomorphism, the normal complement theorem of Burnside, the focal subgroup theorem, the theorem of Schur and Zassenhaus, Z-groups and fixed-point-free automorphisms.

The chapter needs Chapters 4 to 6, and the correspondence theorem of Chapter 5. The chapters after it treat groups with zero, free groups and presentations, abelian groups, solvable and nilpotent groups, the localization of monoids and pointwise operations.
