# Order and algebra

This chapter collects the parts of the library where an order and an algebraic structure meet beyond the ordered groups of Chapter 8.

The first section treats the maps that keep both an order and an operation, such as the ordered monoid homomorphisms and the ordered ring isomorphisms. The second section gives the images of intervals and the bounds of sets under the group operations, the upper sets of an ordered group, and the actions of a monoid that keep an order. The third section makes the intervals of an ordered monoid into a monoid, as in interval arithmetic, and gives the algebraic structure of the unit interval $[0, 1]$ and its neighbours. The fourth section joins the monovariance of Chapter 2 to sums and products, and proves the rearrangement inequality. The fifth section treats the idempotent semirings, in which the sum is a supremum, the Kleene algebras with their star, and the quantales with their residuation.

The chapter needs the ordered monoids and groups of Chapter 8, the monotone maps of Chapter 2, the lattices of Chapter 3, the complete lattices of Chapter 4, the Galois connections of Chapter 6 and the intervals of Chapter 7.
