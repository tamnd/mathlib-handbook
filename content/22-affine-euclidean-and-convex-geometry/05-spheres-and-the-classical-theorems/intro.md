# Spheres and the classical theorems

This chapter proves the classical theorems of Euclidean geometry about circles and triangles, in the form that Mathlib states them. Section 1 defines spheres, the second intersection of a line with a sphere, tangent lines and the power of a point, and proves the theorems of intersecting chords, intersecting secants and the tangent and the secant from one formula. Section 2 proves the inscribed angle theorem with oriented angles, the theorem of Thales, the criterion for four points to be concyclic, the theorem of Ptolemy, and Ptolemy's inequality by inversion in a sphere, and ends with Euler's theorem on quadrilaterals. Section 3 constructs the circumsphere of a simplex, the orthocentre and the Euler line, the nine-point circle, the incentre and the excentres, and proves the formulas of Heron and $\text{area} = r\sigma$.

The chapter needs Chapters 2, 3 and 4: simplices and affine independence, the unoriented angle and the triangle theorems, and the oriented angle in the plane. Section 2 uses the power of a point of Section 1, and Section 3 uses Thales' theorem of Section 2. Mathlib states most of these results for a space of any dimension, and the plane results ask for the instances `Fact (finrank ℝ V = 2)` and `Module.Oriented ℝ V (Fin 2)`.
