# Star algebras

This chapter builds the algebra of star rings beyond the definitions of Chapter 8. Section 1 describes the maps that respect the star: star monoid homomorphisms, non-unital star ring homomorphisms, and unital and non-unital star algebra homomorphisms, with their equivalences. Section 2 describes the substructures that are closed under the star: star subsemirings, star subalgebras and their non-unital forms, the star subalgebra that a set generates, the centre and the centralizer, and the pointwise star of a set. Section 3 studies three kinds of special elements: unitary elements, star projections and pairs of star orthogonal elements. It also proves the CHSH inequality of quantum information in a commutative star ordered algebra. Section 4 puts star structures on the standard constructions: modules and linear maps, products and function types, free algebras, ring quotients, tensor products, the rational numbers, and structures that are carried along an equivalence. Section 5 is the library of star-ordered rings: conjugation by an element, the orthogonality of nonnegative elements, the order on projections, strictly positive elements and the standard examples.

The chapter needs the star rings, the self-adjoint elements and the algebras of Chapter 8, and the ring homomorphisms and subrings of Chapter 1. Section 2 uses the subalgebra generated by a set from Chapter 8. Section 3 uses ordered rings from Chapter 7 for the CHSH inequality. Section 1 comes first, because each later section uses its maps. Sections 2 to 4 can then be read in any order. Section 5 uses the ordered rings of Chapter 7 and the star-ordered rings of Chapter 8.

Volume 7 on linear algebra uses the star modules and the star structures on linear maps of Section 4. Volume 16 builds C*-algebras and the continuous functional calculus on the star algebra homomorphisms of Section 1, the star subalgebras of Section 2, and the unitary elements and projections of Section 3.
