# The polynomial library in one variable

Chapters 5 and 6 use the ring $R[X]$ as a known object. Mathlib builds it in more than one hundred files under `Mathlib/Algebra/Polynomial`, and a reader who proves a fact about polynomials meets this library at each step. This chapter is a guide to it. It treats the library in the order of its dependencies, and it gives the names, the normal forms and the common traps of each part.

Section 1 describes the type `Polynomial R`, its constructors `C`, `X` and `monomial`, the coefficients, the sums over the support and the induction principles. Section 2 treats the degree and the natural degree, the leading coefficient and monic polynomials. Section 3 treats the trailing degree, the erasure of the leading term, the reverse and the mirror of a polynomial. Section 4 treats evaluation, the maps of coefficients, the algebra structure and the polynomials that lift along a ring homomorphism. Section 5 treats division with remainder by a monic polynomial and over a field. Section 6 treats roots and their multiplicities, the multiset of roots and split polynomials, and Descartes' rule of signs and Sturm sequences for real roots. Section 7 treats the derivative, the Hasse derivatives, the Taylor expansion and the expansion $f(X) \mapsto f(X^p)$. Section 8 treats Laurent polynomials, polynomials in two variables and homogenization. Section 9 treats modules over $R[X]$, in particular the module that an endomorphism defines.

The chapter needs the polynomial rings of Volume 6, Chapter 5, and the finitely supported functions of Volume 3. It is a reference chapter: a reader can open a section when a proof needs its part of the library.
