# Subgroups

A subgroup is a subset that is a group under the same product. Section 1 gives the subgroup tests, the lattice `Subgroup G`, the subgroup that a set generates, and the kernel and the range of a homomorphism, with the six subgroups of $S_3$. Section 2 studies images and preimages of subgroups and the cyclic groups: their structure, their subgroups, and the induction principle for a closure. Section 3 describes the library of centers and centralizers of semigroups, monoids and groups, the conjugation map of a normalizer with kernel the centralizer, and the submonoid of left inverses. Section 4 defines the word length and the word metric of a generating family, and proves that a finite set that generates an infinite group grows at least linearly. Section 5 moves submonoids and subgroups to the opposite monoid, an order isomorphism that keeps closures and normality and describes left cosets as orbits of a right action. Section 6 studies saturated submonoids, which hold the factors of their elements, and the support, the pointed and the spanning submonoids of a group. Section 7 treats subsemigroups, the bottom layer of the tower of substructures, with their lattice, closure, directed suprema, images, preimages and products. Section 8 treats submonoids: closure and its induction principle, directed suprema, the powers of an element, products of generators, and the indecomposable members of a family. Section 9 moves submonoids along homomorphisms, with images, preimages, ranges, kernels and products, and connects submonoids of $M$ with subgroups of $M^\times$. Section 10 gives the center and centralizers as sets, conjugate elements and conjugacy classes, and the commutator bracket of a group.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 and 2. Chapter 4 counts the cosets of a subgroup, and Chapter 5 asks which subgroups are normal.
