# Normed rings and Banach algebras

A normed ring is a ring with a submultiplicative norm, and a Banach algebra is a complete normed algebra over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$. The bounded operators on a Banach space and the square matrices are the main examples, and the theory of these algebras is the setting for the spectrum, the exponential and the functional calculus. Section 1 gives the classes of normed rings, the classes `NormOneClass` and `NormMulClass`, and the products of absolutely convergent series. Section 2 studies normed fields, their unit balls and spheres, and the continuity of the roots of a polynomial. Section 3 defines the spectrum and the spectral radius, proves the formula of Gelfand for the spectral radius, and gives two forms of the theorem of Gelfand and Mazur. Section 4 defines the exponential and the logarithm by their series, applies the exponential to matrices, and puts the norm on the quaternions. Section 5 gives the norms on the unitization of an algebra without a unit and on the square zero extensions, with the dual numbers as an example.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 and 2. Section 3 also uses the weak star topology of Chapter 5, and Section 4 uses the inner product spaces of Chapter 6. Chapter 12 continues the theory with $C^*$ algebras.
