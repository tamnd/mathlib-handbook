# Abelian groups

This chapter gives the library of torsion groups, divisible groups, free abelian groups and finite abelian groups. Section 1 gives torsion and torsion-free commutative groups, primary components, divisible groups and the divisible hull of a monoid. Section 2 gives the free abelian group with its universal property, the structure theorem for finitely generated commutative groups, and the duality of finite commutative groups.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 to 5, and Section 2 also uses the free groups of Chapter 10.
