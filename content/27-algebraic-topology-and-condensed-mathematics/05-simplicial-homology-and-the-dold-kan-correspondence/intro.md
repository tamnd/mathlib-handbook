# Simplicial homology and the Dold-Kan correspondence

This chapter turns simplicial objects into chain complexes and studies the homology that results. Section 1 defines the alternating face map complex and the normalized Moore complex of a simplicial object. It defines the homology of a simplicial set with coefficients in an object of a preadditive category, computes it in degree zero and for the boundary of the 2-simplex, and shows that degenerate simplices do not change it. It then builds the topological simplex, the singular simplicial set and geometric realization, defines singular homology, and proves homotopy invariance and the long exact sequence of a pair. Section 2 states the Eilenberg-Steenrod axioms for homology theories on topological pairs, as Mathlib does, and derives first consequences from them. Section 3 proves the Dold-Kan correspondence: the normalized Moore complex is an equivalence between simplicial objects and chain complexes in degrees at least zero, for abelian and pseudoabelian categories, and after idempotent completion for any preadditive category.

The chapter needs the limits, adjunctions and additive categories of Volume 24, the abelian categories of Volume 25, and the complexes, homology and homotopies of Volume 26, Chapters 1 to 3. From this volume it needs the homotopies of Chapter 1, the simplex category, the simplicial objects and the simplicial sets of Chapter 3, and the pairs of simplicial sets of Chapter 4.

Chapter 6 places these results in the frame of model categories, where weak equivalences, such as maps that induce isomorphisms on homology, become isomorphisms in a homotopy category. Its cell complexes are the spaces for which the Eilenberg-Steenrod axioms of Section 2 determine homology from the value on a point. Chapter 8 defines condensed abelian groups, an abelian category to which the constructions of this chapter apply.
