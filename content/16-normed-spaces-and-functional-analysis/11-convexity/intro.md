# Convexity

Convex sets and convex functions appear in each part of functional analysis: the unit ball of a normed space is convex, the theorem of Hahn-Banach separates convex sets, and the nearest point theorem of Chapter 7 needs a convex set. This chapter collects the convexity library of Mathlib. Section 1 defines segments, convex and star convex sets, convex hulls, convex combinations and joins, and proves the separation theorem of Stone. Section 2 studies convex functions on convex sets, the inequality of Jensen, slopes, quasiconvex and strongly convex functions, and the minima of convex functions. Section 3 treats convex functions of one real variable: the tests with the first and second derivatives, the convexity of the exponential, the logarithm and the powers, and the integral form of Jensen. Section 4 studies the topology of convex sets: closure and interior, path connectedness and contractibility, convex bodies, the measure of the frontier, continuous selections and the homeomorphism of a convex body with the closed ball. Section 5 defines proper cones and their duals, proves the bipolar theorem and the lemma of Farkas, and proves the theorem of Birkhoff on doubly stochastic matrices. Section 6 studies strictly convex and uniformly convex spaces, the equality case of the triangle inequality and the affine isometries.

The chapter needs Chapter 1 and the linear algebra of Volume 7. Sections 2 to 6 also use the topology of Volume 13. Section 4 uses the gauge of Chapter 5, Section 5 uses the separation theorems of Chapter 5 and the inner product of Chapter 6, and Section 6 uses inner product spaces for its main example.
