# Extended numbers: NNReal, ENNReal, ENat and EReal

Measure theory needs lengths that are not negative and can be infinite, and combinatorics needs sizes that can be infinite. This chapter adds a top element, and for `EReal` also a bottom element, to `ℝ≥0`, `ℕ` and `ℝ`. The first section shows `NNReal` and `ENNReal`, the rules of their arithmetic with `⊤`, and the theorem that each series in `ℝ≥0∞` has a sum. The second section shows `ENat` and `EReal`, built from `WithTop` and `WithBot`. The third section describes the library of division and inverses in `ℝ≥0∞`, the maps to `ℝ` and `ℝ≥0`, the scalar actions of `ℝ≥0` and the finite sums and products. The fourth section describes the Hölder triples and the conjugate exponents in `ℝ≥0∞`, `ℝ` and `ℝ≥0`, the arithmetic of the exponents of $L^p$ spaces. The fifth section describes the complete lattice `ℕ∞` with its sums and powers, the cast from `ℕ∞` to `ℝ≥0∞`, the inverse and the division of `EReal`, and the theorem that `EReal` is the Dedekind completion of `ℚ`. In each type, the casts back to `ℕ` or `ℝ` send the infinite elements to the junk value $0$, so the lemmas about the casts state the hypotheses `≠ ⊤` and `≠ ⊥`.
