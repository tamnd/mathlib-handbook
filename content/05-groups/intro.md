# Groups

This volume teaches group theory and its form in Mathlib, from the axioms to the Sylow theorems. Each theorem has a complete proof on paper, the Mathlib name of the result, and computed examples in small groups: $\mathbb{Z}/n$, the Klein group, $S_3$, $S_4$, $A_4$ and the dihedral groups.

Chapters 1 to 3 give the language: monoids, groups and units, homomorphisms and isomorphisms, subgroups and cyclic groups. Chapter 4 proves the theorem of Lagrange and studies the order of an element. Chapter 5 builds quotient groups and proves the three isomorphism theorems. Chapter 6 introduces group actions, with orbit-stabilizer and the class equation. Chapter 7 studies permutations, the sign and the cycle type. Chapter 8 proves the theorem of Cauchy and the three Sylow theorems, and applies them to groups of small order.

Read Volume 1 first: this volume uses its classes, sets, equivalences and finite cardinalities. The divisibility, $\gcd$ and congruences of the integers, from Volume 2, are used from Chapter 4 on. Read the chapters in order. Later volumes on rings, modules and fields use the quotients of Chapter 5, the actions of Chapter 6 and the Sylow theorems of Chapter 8. Each chapter has a companion file of Lean examples, exercises and solutions in tamnd/mathlib-lean.
