# Constructions and finiteness of modules

This chapter collects the constructions of modules that commutative algebra and homological algebra use, and the invariants that measure how far a module is from a finite free module. Section 1 builds the localization $S^{-1}M$ of a module at a multiplicative set and the predicate `IsLocalizedModule` that characterizes it. Section 2 builds the direct limit of a directed system of modules, of rings and of other algebraic structures, and shows that each module is the direct limit of its finitely generated submodules. Section 3 describes a module by generators and relations, and studies the finitely presented modules. Section 4 studies the projective, injective and stably free modules, the character module $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(M, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ and the snake lemma. Section 5 defines the least number of generators of a submodule, the linear maps with a finitely generated range, and the index $\dim \ker f - \dim \operatorname{coker} f$ of a linear map.

The chapter needs the modules, submodules, quotients and linear equivalences of Chapter 1, the bases and the rank of Chapter 2, and the commutative rings and ideals of Volume 6. The tensor product appears in a few statements only.
