# Subgroups

A subgroup is a subset that is a group under the same product. Section 1 gives the subgroup tests, the lattice `Subgroup G`, the subgroup that a set generates, and the kernel and the range of a homomorphism, with the six subgroups of $S_3$. Section 2 studies images and preimages of subgroups and the cyclic groups: their structure, their subgroups, and the induction principle for a closure.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 and 2. Chapter 4 counts the cosets of a subgroup, and Chapter 5 asks which subgroups are normal.
