# Metaprogramming support

Mathlib is also a library of programs: the tactics, attributes, notations and commands that a user calls in a proof are written in Lean on top of a small set of utilities. This chapter explains these utilities for a user who meets them through the tactics and the goal view. The first section describes how Mathlib reads and builds terms, names and environments, with expressions for numerals, the local context of a goal and the information trees of the editor. The second section explains the atoms behind `ring` and `abel`, the refined discrimination trees behind `rw??` and `fun_prop`, and the helpers that let a tactic rewrite at a location or call a discharger. The third section explains `notation3`, superscripts and the printing of goals, together with the commands that count heartbeats, find `sorry` and list new declarations.
