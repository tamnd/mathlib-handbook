# Introduction

This volume is about the tactics of Mathlib. Each section takes one tactic or a small group of tactics. It shows the tactic on a worked goal, and it tells what the tactic cannot do. The examples come from the companion `tamnd/mathlib-lean`, and each one builds against the Mathlib commit of the edition.

Read this volume with any other volume. It needs only Volume 0. The order of the chapters goes from the tactics that rewrite a goal, to the tactics that decide a goal, to the tactics that give a proof its structure.
