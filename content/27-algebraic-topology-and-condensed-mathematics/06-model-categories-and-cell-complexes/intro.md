# Model categories and cell complexes

This chapter gives two frames for homotopy theory. Section 1 defines model categories as Mathlib does: a category with weak equivalences, fibrations and cofibrations that satisfies the axioms of Quillen. It defines cofibrant and fibrant objects, cylinder and path objects, and left and right homotopy. It proves the retract argument, the factorization lemma of K. S. Brown, the agreement of the two homotopy relations for a cofibrant source and a fibrant target, and the Whitehead theorem for bifibrant objects. It then gives the fundamental lemma, which identifies maps in the homotopy category with homotopy classes. Section 2 defines relative cell complexes in any category, abstract CW complexes in `TopCat`, and classical CW complexes in the sense of Whitehead. It proves the lifting property of cell complexes and the basic facts about cells, skeletons, finite complexes, subcomplexes and the graph of the $1$-skeleton.

The chapter needs the limits and adjunctions of Volume 24, the lifting properties and the localizations of Volume 25, and the topological spaces and compact sets of Volume 13. From this volume it needs the homotopies of Chapter 1, the simplicial sets of Chapter 3, the Kan complexes of Chapter 4 and the homology of Chapter 5.

In this edition the only concrete model structure in Mathlib is the injective structure on bounded below cochain complexes. Mathlib has no model structure on simplicial sets or on spaces, and it has no comparison between the abstract and the classical CW complexes. Chapter 7 studies the categories of spaces in which these constructions take place, and Chapter 8 defines condensed sets and condensed abelian groups.
