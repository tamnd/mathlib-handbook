# Numbers

This volume shows the number types of Mathlib: `ℕ`, `ℤ`, `ℚ`, `ℝ`, the residues `Fin n` and `ZMod n`, and the extended types `NNReal`, `ENNReal` and `ENat`. For each type, it shows how Lean builds it, the junk values of its operations and the main lemmas. One chapter shows the casts between the types, because a statement that mixes two number types has casts in it. Read Volume 1 first. The chapters on `ℕ`, `ℤ` and `ℚ` go in order, and the later chapters use them. The complex numbers and the binary numbers of `Mathlib/Data/Num` are not in this draft. A later draft adds them.
