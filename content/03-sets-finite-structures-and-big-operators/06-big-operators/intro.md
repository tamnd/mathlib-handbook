# Big operators

A finite sum $\sum_{i \in s} f(i)$ and a finite product $\prod_{i \in s} f(i)$ are the big operators. Mathlib defines them over a finset, with the notation `∑ i ∈ s, f i` and `∏ i ∈ s, f i`. One definition serves every commutative monoid, and each rule for products has a twin for sums with the same proof. This chapter gives the definitions, the rules of computation and the standard identities, with complete proofs and computed values.

Section 1 defines `Finset.sum` and `Finset.prod` through multisets, and it proves the basic rules: the empty sum, the insert rule, the induction on `Finset.range`, linearity, constants and the card as a sum of ones. Section 2 gives the algebra of finite sums: reindexing by a bijection, splitting over a disjoint union, the exchange of the order of summation (the theorem of Fubini for finite sums), telescoping and the geometric sum, the order rules, the inequality of Cauchy and Schwarz and the sums of an `if`. Section 3 uses this algebra for the standard identities and for counting: the binomial theorem and the rule of Pascal, the rows of Pascal, the hockey stick, the sums of powers, double counting and the handshake, inclusion and exclusion, and the number of derangements.

The chapter needs the multisets of Chapter 2, the finsets and the card of Chapter 3, and the finite types and `Finset.univ` of Chapter 4. Chapter 7 uses the big operators for the sums over the support of a finitely supported function. The volume on combinatorics continues the counting of Section 3, and the volumes on rings and on analysis use the rules of Sections 1 and 2 at each step.
