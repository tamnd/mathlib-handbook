# Permutations and the sign

The permutations of a finite set form the group `Equiv.Perm α`. Section 1 defines the sign by inversions, proves that it is a homomorphism, shows that the transpositions generate, and defines the alternating group $A_n$ of index $2$. Section 2 proves the cycle decomposition, computes the order and the sign from the cycle type, shows that conjugacy in $S_n$ is equality of cycle types, and proves that the $3$-cycles generate $A_n$.

The chapter needs Chapters 4 to 6. The groups $S_4$ and $A_4$ are the main examples of Chapter 8, and the simplicity of $A_n$ for $n \ge 5$ starts from the $3$-cycles.
