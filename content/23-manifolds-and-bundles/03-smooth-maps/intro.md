# Smooth maps

A map between manifolds is $C^n$ when it is continuous and $C^n$ in charts. This chapter makes that definition precise and builds its theory. It uses the extended charts of Chapter 2 to read a map $f : M \to M'$ as a map between subsets of normed spaces, and the classes $C^n$ of Volume 17 to test it. Here $n$ is a natural number, $\infty$ or $\omega$.

Section 1 defines the four predicates `ContMDiffWithinAt`, `ContMDiffAt`, `ContMDiffOn` and `ContMDiff`. The definition goes through the local invariant properties of Chapter 1, so that the independence of the charts comes from the general theorem of that chapter. The section proves the formula in extended charts, the invariance of the property under the groupoid of $C^n$ maps, the composition rule and the locality of the notion. Section 2 gives the basic smooth maps: charts and their inverses, projections and pairs on products, families indexed by a type, and maps between normed spaces, where the new notion agrees with the old one. It also defines the bundled type of $C^n$ maps. Section 3 defines diffeomorphisms and local diffeomorphisms. A diffeomorphism is a bijection that is $C^n$ in both directions, and a local diffeomorphism is a map that is a diffeomorphism near each point. The section proves that local diffeomorphisms are open maps, and that a bijective local diffeomorphism is a diffeomorphism. It ends with a short look at diffeological spaces, a generalisation of manifolds in the library.

The chapter needs Chapters 1 and 2 and the classes $C^n$ of Volume 17, Chapter 4. Chapter 4 uses smooth maps to define the derivative, and Chapter 5 uses them for the smooth structure on bundles.
