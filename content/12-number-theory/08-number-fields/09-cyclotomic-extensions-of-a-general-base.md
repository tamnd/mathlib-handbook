## Cyclotomic extensions of a general base

### Overview

Section 5 studies the fields $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$. The results there rest on a general theory: an extension $B$ of a ring $A$ generated by roots of unity of given orders. This theory works over any base, such as $\mathbb{Q}$, a number field, a finite field, or $\mathbb{Z}$ itself. It gives the Galois group, the power bases with generator $\zeta$ or $\zeta - 1$, the norms of $\zeta$ and $\zeta - 1$, and the discriminants of prime power cyclotomic extensions. In each statement over a field $K$, the hypothesis that the cyclotomic polynomial $\Phi_n$ is irreducible over $K$ takes the place of the special facts about $\mathbb{Q}$.

The module `Mathlib.NumberTheory.Cyclotomic.Basic` defines the class and proves the structural facts. The module `Mathlib.NumberTheory.Cyclotomic.PrimitiveRoots` chooses a primitive root and computes power bases and norms. The module `Mathlib.NumberTheory.Cyclotomic.Gal` identifies the Galois group, `Mathlib.NumberTheory.Cyclotomic.Discriminant` computes discriminants, and `Mathlib.NumberTheory.Cyclotomic.CyclotomicCharacter` defines the cyclotomic character of a ring automorphism. Roots of unity, primitive roots and cyclotomic polynomials are in Chapter 8 of Volume 11, and power bases are in Chapter 2 of Volume 7.

### The library around it

#### The class

**Definition.** Let $S$ be a set of natural numbers and $B$ an $A$-algebra. Then `IsCyclotomicExtension S A B` holds when

1. for each $n \in S$ with $n \neq 0$ there is a primitive $n$-th root of unity in $B$, which is `IsCyclotomicExtension.exists_isPrimitiveRoot`;
2. $B$ is generated as an $A$-algebra by the elements $b$ with $b^n = 1$ for some nonzero $n \in S$, which is `IsCyclotomicExtension.adjoin_roots`.

The forms `isCyclotomicExtension_iff` and `IsCyclotomicExtension.iff_adjoin_eq_top` state the class as a conjunction. The case of one order is the main one, with `IsCyclotomicExtension.iff_singleton`. Some degenerate cases: `IsCyclotomicExtension.isCyclotomicExtension_zero_iff` says that the class with $S = \{0\}$ holds when `algebraMap A B` is surjective, and `IsCyclotomicExtension.singleton_one` says that an extension for $\{1\}$ is trivial.

The model is `CyclotomicField n K`, the splitting field of $\Phi_n$ over $K$, with the instance `CyclotomicField.isCyclotomicExtension` when $n$ is nonzero in $K$. For a domain $A$ with fraction field $K$, `CyclotomicRing n A K` is the subalgebra of `CyclotomicField n K` generated over $A$ by the roots of unity, with the instance `CyclotomicRing.isCyclotomicExtension`. If $\zeta$ is a primitive $n$-th root in $B$, then `IsPrimitiveRoot.adjoin_isCyclotomicExtension` says that $A[\zeta]$ is a cyclotomic extension for $\{n\}$.

#### Structure

The class behaves well under the usual operations.

| Lean name | Statement |
|---|---|
| `IsCyclotomicExtension.trans` | an $S$-extension of a $T$-extension is an $S \cup T$-extension |
| `IsCyclotomicExtension.union_right` | an $S \cup T$-extension is a $T$-extension of the $S$-part |
| `IsCyclotomicExtension.iff_union_of_dvd` | adding $n$ to $S$ changes nothing if $n$ divides some element of $S$ |
| `IsCyclotomicExtension.equiv` | the class moves along an $A$-algebra isomorphism |
| `IsCyclotomicExtension.finite` | $B$ is a finite $A$-module when $S$ is finite and $B$ is a domain |
| `IsCyclotomicExtension.numberField` | a finite cyclotomic extension of a number field is a number field |

Over a field $K$, an extension $L$ with `IsCyclotomicExtension {n} K L` is the splitting field of $X^n - 1$ and of $\Phi_n$, by `IsCyclotomicExtension.isSplittingField_X_pow_sub_one` and `IsCyclotomicExtension.splitting_field_cyclotomic`. So it is Galois, `IsCyclotomicExtension.isGalois`, and its Galois group is abelian, `IsCyclotomicExtension.isAbelianGalois`. Two cyclotomic extensions of $K$ for the same $S$ are isomorphic, by `IsCyclotomicExtension.algEquiv`.

#### The primitive root and its power bases

For `IsCyclotomicExtension {n} A B`, the element `IsCyclotomicExtension.zeta n A B` is a chosen primitive $n$-th root of unity, with `IsCyclotomicExtension.zeta_spec`. Most lemmas take an arbitrary $\zeta$ with `hζ : IsPrimitiveRoot ζ n` instead, which is more flexible.

Over a field $K$ with $\Phi_n$ irreducible, the minimal polynomial of $\zeta$ is $\Phi_n$. So $1, \zeta, \dots, \zeta^{\varphi(n) - 1}$ is a basis and $[L : K] = \varphi(n)$, which is `IsCyclotomicExtension.finrank`. The bundled basis is `IsPrimitiveRoot.powerBasis K hζ`, with `IsPrimitiveRoot.powerBasis_gen` and `IsPrimitiveRoot.powerBasis_dim`. The basis with the generator $\zeta - 1$ is `IsPrimitiveRoot.subOnePowerBasis K hζ`, and its minimal polynomial is $\Phi_n(X + 1)$ by `IsPrimitiveRoot.minpoly_sub_one_eq_cyclotomic_comp`. The embeddings of $L$ into a domain $C$ over $K$ correspond to the primitive $n$-th roots in $C$, by `IsPrimitiveRoot.embeddingsEquivPrimitiveRoots`.

#### Norms

Let $\Phi_n$ be irreducible over $K$. The conjugates of $\zeta$ are the primitive $n$-th roots of unity, so $N(\zeta - 1) = \prod_{\zeta'}(\zeta' - 1) = \pm\Phi_n(1)$ and $N(\zeta)$ is the product of all primitive roots.

| Lean name | Statement |
|---|---|
| `IsPrimitiveRoot.norm_eq_one` | $N(\zeta) = 1$ for $n \neq 2$ |
| `IsPrimitiveRoot.norm_eq_neg_one_pow` | $N(-1) = (-1)^{[L : K]}$ |
| `IsPrimitiveRoot.sub_one_norm_eq_eval_cyclotomic` | $N(\zeta - 1) = \Phi_n(1)$ for $n > 2$ |
| `IsPrimitiveRoot.sub_one_norm_isPrimePow` | $N(\zeta - 1) = p$ for $n = p^k \neq 2$ |
| `IsPrimitiveRoot.norm_sub_one_of_prime_ne_two` | $N(\zeta - 1) = p$ for $n = p^{k+1}$, $p$ odd |
| `IsPrimitiveRoot.norm_sub_one_two` | $N(\zeta - 1) = 2$ for $n = 2^k$, $k \geq 2$ |

*Proof of the value $p$.* For $n = p^k$, $\Phi_n(X) = 1 + X^{p^{k-1}} + \cdots + X^{(p-1)p^{k-1}}$, so $\Phi_n(1) = p$. For $n > 2$ the degree $\varphi(n)$ is even, so the sign is $+$. $\square$

The forms with the chosen root have the prefix `IsCyclotomicExtension`, such as `IsCyclotomicExtension.norm_zeta_sub_one_of_prime_ne_two` and `IsCyclotomicExtension.norm_zeta_eq_one`. The norms to $\mathbb{Z}$ in Section 5 come from these. A bound in the other direction is `IsPrimitiveRoot.lcm_totient_le_finrank`: if $L$ has primitive roots of orders $p$ and $q$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{lcm}(p, q)}$ is irreducible, then $\varphi(\mathrm{lcm}(p, q)) \leq [L : K]$.

#### The Galois group

**Theorem.** Let $L$ be a cyclotomic extension of $K$ for $\{n\}$ and let $\Phi_n$ be irreducible over $K$. Then $\sigma \mapsto a_\sigma$, with $\sigma(\zeta) = \zeta^{a_\sigma}$, is an isomorphism $\mathrm{Aut}_K(L) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^\times$.

In Lean this is `IsCyclotomicExtension.autEquivPow`, applied to $L$ and a proof of the irreducibility. It is built from `IsPrimitiveRoot.autToPow` of Volume 11, which is injective by `IsPrimitiveRoot.autToPow_injective`. The defining property for all roots of unity is `IsCyclotomicExtension.autEquivPow_apply_of_pow_eq_one`: if $x^n = 1$, then $\sigma(x) = x^{a_\sigma}$. Conversely, `IsCyclotomicExtension.fromZetaAut` is the automorphism that sends a fixed root to a given primitive root. The same group with the Galois group of a polynomial is `galCyclotomicEquivUnitsZMod` for $\Phi_n$ and `galXPowEquivUnitsZMod` for $X^n - 1$.

The irreducibility of $\Phi_n$ is needed. Over $\mathbb{F}_p$ with $p \nmid n$, the Galois group of $\Phi_n$ is the cyclic subgroup of $(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})^\times$ generated by $p$. Over $\mathbb{Q}$ the polynomial is irreducible, and the isomorphism becomes `IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.galEquivZMod` of Section 5.

#### Discriminants

For $n = p^k$ with $\Phi_n$ irreducible over $K$, the discriminant of the power basis of $\zeta$ is
$$\mathrm{disc}(1, \zeta, \dots, \zeta^{\varphi(n) - 1}) = (-1)^{\varphi(n)/2}\,p^{p^{k-1}((p - 1)k - 1)}.$$
This is `IsCyclotomicExtension.discr_prime_pow`, with `IsCyclotomicExtension.discr_prime_pow_ne_two` for the form with $k + 1$ and `IsCyclotomicExtension.discr_odd_prime` for $n = p$ odd, where the value is $(-1)^{(p-1)/2}p^{p-2}$. The weaker `IsCyclotomicExtension.discr_prime_pow_eq_unit_mul_pow` says only that the discriminant is a unit of $K$ times a power of $p$, and this is the form the proof of $\mathcal{O}_K = \mathbb{Z}[\zeta]$ in Section 5 uses. `IsPrimitiveRoot.discr_zeta_eq_discr_zeta_sub_one` says that over $\mathbb{Q}$ the bases of $\zeta$ and $\zeta - 1$ have the same discriminant, because the change of basis matrix is unipotent.

#### The cyclotomic character

Let $L$ be a domain. A ring automorphism $g$ of $L$ permutes the $n$-th roots of unity. If there are $n$ of them, it acts on them as $t \mapsto t^{a}$ for a unit $a$ modulo $n$. This defines `modularCyclotomicCharacter`, a homomorphism `(L ≃+* L) →* (ZMod n)ˣ`, whose second argument says that the group of $n$-th roots of unity has $n$ elements. Its property is `modularCyclotomicCharacter.spec`, and `IsPrimitiveRoot.autToPow_eq_modularCyclotomicCharacter` relates it to `IsPrimitiveRoot.autToPow`. For a prime $p$ and a domain with enough roots of unity of all orders $p^i$, the characters modulo $p^i$ are compatible, and their limit is the $p$-adic cyclotomic character `cyclotomicCharacter L p : (L ≃+* L) →* ℤ_[p]ˣ`, with `cyclotomicCharacter.spec`. Restricted to a Galois group with the Krull topology it is continuous, `cyclotomicCharacter.continuous`. The $p$-adic integers are in Chapter 5.

#### Common errors

1. Most statements need `Irreducible (Polynomial.cyclotomic n K)` as an explicit argument. Over $\mathbb{Q}$ it is `Polynomial.cyclotomic.irreducible_rat`. Over other fields it can be false, and then the degree and the Galois group are smaller.
2. `IsCyclotomicExtension {n} A B` asks for a primitive $n$-th root in $B$. In a domain of characteristic $p$ with $p \mid n$ there is none, so the class with $n = p$ cannot hold over $\mathbb{F}_p$ when $B$ is a field.
3. `IsCyclotomicExtension.zeta n A B` is a chosen root. A lemma about a given $\zeta$ with `IsPrimitiveRoot ζ n` does not apply to the chosen root without `IsCyclotomicExtension.zeta_spec`.

### Exercises

1. (A) Use `IsCyclotomicExtension.iff_union_of_dvd` to show that a cyclotomic extension for $\{2, 6\}$ is one for $\{6\}$.
2. (A) Compute $N(\zeta_9 - 1)$ and the discriminant of the power basis of $\zeta_9$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ from the formulas of this section.
3. (B) Show that $[\mathbb{F}_2(\zeta_7) : \mathbb{F}_2] = 3$, and explain why `IsCyclotomicExtension.finrank` does not apply there.
4. (B) Prove `IsPrimitiveRoot.norm_eq_one` for odd $n > 1$ by pairing each primitive root with its inverse.
5. (C) Let $L$ be a cyclotomic extension of $K$ for $\{n\}$. Show that a subfield $F$ with $K \subseteq F \subseteq L$ is Galois over $K$, with `IsCyclotomicExtension.isAbelianGalois`.
